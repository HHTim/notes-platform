動詞變成的形容詞

分詞當形容詞：-ing 是主動，過去分詞是被動

分詞是動詞的兩種變形：現在分詞就是 -ing 形（rising、remaining），過去分詞是規則動詞加 -ed（updated、signed）、不規則動詞各有各的樣子（broken、written、chosen）。它們跟〈不定詞與動名詞〉那課的 to＋原形、-ing 一樣，都不能自己當句子的主要動詞。這類形式叫非限定形式，不顯示時態、人稱或單複數，典型的有三種：出處

  • 不定詞（帶 to 或不帶 to）：to go、go
  • -ing 形：going
  • -ed 形（過去分詞）：gone

分詞最常見的用法是當形容詞，規則動詞、不規則動詞的 -ing 形與 -ed 形都可以：boiling water（滾水）、smoked salmon（燻鮭魚）。出處 兩種怎麼選：出處

  • 現在分詞（-ing）：意思接近主動的動詞。
  • 過去分詞：通常是被動的意思。

問一句：這個名詞是「自己在做」這個動作，還是「被人做了」？自己做用 -ing，被做用過去分詞。

表示情緒的動詞（interest、confuse、satisfy、disappoint、excite、bore、surprise……）變成形容詞時，-ing 和 -ed 意思不同：出處出處

人也可以用 -ing，但意思不一樣

-ing 不是只能說東西。一個人或東西是 interesting，就是他讓你覺得 interested。出處 所以 He was boring. 文法沒錯，意思是「他這個人很無聊（讓別人覺得無聊）」，不是「他覺得無聊」。

  • ✗ He didn't enjoy the lesson because he was boring. → ✓ …because he was bored.：他不喜歡那堂課，是因為他覺得無聊，錯在意思。出處

放前面、放後面

分詞放名詞前後，以及它和關係子句的關係

分詞可以放在名詞前面，也可以放在名詞後面，例如 Who is that man standing over there? 的 standing 就放在 man 後面。出處 常見的情況是：

  • 只有一個分詞：放名詞前面，the attached file。
  • 分詞後面還帶著受詞或介系詞片語：整串放名詞後面，the file attached to this e-mail、the people attending the meeting。

放在名詞後面的分詞片語，可以看成關係子句的縮短版（關係子句是 who、which、that 開頭、修飾前面名詞的子句）。被修飾的名詞在句子裡當主詞、當受詞都可以。下面兩個例子一個主動、一個被動，而且第二個的 the form 是 fill in 的受詞：

  • The man sitting on the sofa over there is Simon’s brother.＝The man who is sitting…（主動 → -ing）
  • Don’t forget to fill in the form attached to the letter.＝…which is attached to the letter.（被動 → 過去分詞）出處

主動：把關係子句的動詞改成 -ing（who is sitting → sitting、anyone who wishes → anyone wishing）。被動：拿掉 who／which＋be，留下過去分詞（which is attached → attached）。

名詞後面的 -ing 片語說的是正在進行、經常性的動作或狀態。過去已經做完的一次性動作，例如 the engineer who designed the system（設計了這套系統的工程師），保留關係子句，不縮成 designing：the engineer designing the system 會變成「正在設計這套系統的工程師」。

關係子句本身怎麼寫，放在〈關係詞〉這課。

有些過去分詞習慣放在名詞後面，不放前面：出處

  • ✗ the shown illustrations → ✓ the illustrations shown

  • Orders placed after 3 p.m. will be shipped the next day.（下午三點後下的訂單隔天出貨。）＝which are placed
  • The woman leading the project used to work in Singapore.（帶這個專案的那位女士以前在新加坡工作。）

  • The problems mentioned in your e-mail have been fixed.（您信中提到的問題已經修好了。）

把副詞子句縮短

分詞構句：-ing、過去分詞、having＋過去分詞

分詞構句（participle clause）是把「when、because、after 這類連接詞開頭的子句」縮成一個分詞片語，用更精簡的方式說同樣的資訊。出處

  • 條件：分詞和主要子句的動詞主詞相同，才能用。例：Waiting for Ellie, I made some tea.＝While I was waiting for Ellie, I made some tea.
  • 時態：分詞構句沒有自己的時態，時態看主要子句。
  • 用在哪裡：主要用在書面文字。

縮短的步驟：

  • 第一步，確認兩個子句主詞相同。主詞不同還硬縮，就會出現下一段講的錯誤。
  • 第二步，拿掉主詞（連接詞可以拿掉；when、while、before、after 也可以留著）。
  • 第三步，動詞改成分詞：主動用 -ing；被動用過去分詞（be 動詞一起拿掉）；比主要子句先完成的動作用 having＋過去分詞。否定把 not 放最前面。
種類原本的句子分詞構句
主動 → -ingWhen I reviewed the report, I found two errors.Reviewing the report, I found two errors.／When reviewing the report, I found…
被動 → 過去分詞The company was founded in 1998. It now has 500 employees.Founded in 1998, the company now has 500 employees.
先完成 → having＋過去分詞After she had finished the project, she took a week off.Having finished the project, she took a week off.
否定 → not 放前面Because he didn't know the address, he called the office.Not knowing the address, he called the office.
結果 → , -ingThe storm closed the airport, and this caused long delays.The storm closed the airport, causing long delays.

出處：英國文化協會、劍橋

表格之外再補幾點：

  • 現在分詞構句能表示的關係：動作的結果（The bomb exploded, destroying the building.）、原因、同時發生的動作。
  • 過去分詞構句：Worried by the news, she called the hospital.、Helped by local volunteers, staff at the museum have spent many years sorting and cataloguing more than 100,000 photographs.
  • 完成式分詞構句（having＋過去分詞）：表示它描述的動作，在主要子句的動作之前已經完成。After having spent six hours at the hospital, they eventually came home.
  • 前面可以加的字：分詞構句也常接在 before、after、instead of、on、since、when、while 這類連接詞和介系詞後面。
  • 否定：Not wanting to hurt his feelings, I avoided the question.
  • When reviewing the contract, please check the payment terms.（審合約時請檢查付款條件。）
  • Having finished the audit, the team sent its report to the board.（查核做完後，團隊把報告送交董事會。）
  • Founded in 1985, the firm now has offices in twelve countries.（這家公司 1985 年創立，現在在十二個國家設有辦公室。）
  • Encouraged by the strong sales, the company opened two more stores.（受到業績亮眼的鼓舞，公司又開了兩家店。）
  • Not having received the invoice, we could not process the payment.（因為沒收到發票，我們無法付款。）
  • The new system processes orders automatically, saving us several hours a day.（新系統自動處理訂單，每天替我們省下好幾個小時。）

主詞要對得上

懸垂分詞：分詞構句最常見的錯

上一段說，分詞構句用在分詞和主要子句的動詞主詞相同的時候；非限定子句一般也常用在主詞跟主要子句的主詞相同時。出處 所以讀的人會把逗號後面的主詞當成分詞的主詞。主詞對不上，句子就會變成別的意思，這種錯誤叫懸垂分詞（dangling participle，分詞「懸」在那裡，找不到自己的主詞）：

主詞對不上
  • Walking into the office, the phone started ringing.
    變成「電話走進辦公室」。

改成主詞相同，或還原成子句

    或 As I walked into the office, the phone started ringing.
  • Having reviewed the proposal, the board approved the budget.
  • Located near the station, the hotel is popular with travelers.

不要誤判的一種：Given the delay, …、Including tax, …、Following the meeting, …、Regarding your order, … 開頭的句子不算懸垂分詞，因為 given、including、following、regarding 在辭典裡都另外列為介系詞（見下一段），用法跟 after、about 一樣，不需要跟主要子句的主詞對上。

下面哪一句文法正確？

  • Arriving at the hotel, our room was not ready.
  • Arriving at the hotel, we found that our room was not ready.
  • Arrived at the hotel, we found that our room was not ready.
  • Arriving at the hotel, it was clear that our room was not ready.

抵達飯店的是「我們」，主要子句的主詞要是 we。our room、it 都不會抵達飯店；arrive 是不及物動詞，沒有被動的 Arrived。

______ the proposal, the board approved the budget.

  • Having been reviewed
  • Reviewed
  • Review
  • Having reviewed

董事會先審提案、再核准預算：主動、先完成，用 Having reviewed。被動的 Having been reviewed、Reviewed 會變成「董事會被審」。

Having checked all the figures, ______.

  • the accountant signed the report
  • the report was signed by the accountant
  • the report looked correct
  • there were no errors

核對數字的是會計，逗號後面的主詞要是 the accountant。其他三個的主詞是 the report、there，都不會核對數字。

Built in 2010, ______.

  • tenants like the office tower
  • the office tower has a modern lobby
  • many companies rent space in the office tower
  • it is easy to rent space in the office tower

被興建的是大樓，逗號後面的主詞要是 the office tower。tenants、many companies、it 都不是 2010 年被蓋好的東西。

下面哪一句有懸垂分詞的錯誤？

  • Including tax, the total is $120.

在等公車的是人，不是手機電池：主詞對不上。Given、Including 在這裡是介系詞，不用跟主詞對上。

  • it was clear to everyone that the projector was broken

走進會議室的是吳小姐，逗號後面的主詞要是 Ms. Wu。投影機和 it 都不會走路。

商業書信的老面孔

多益常見的分詞片語：attached、enclosed、including、included、following、regarding、given

這幾個字在多益的電子郵件、公告、廣告裡很常見。有的仍是分詞，有的在辭典裡已經另外列成介系詞，用法要分開記：

字用法例句
attached（附在電子郵件裡）附上的Please see the attached file.／the file attached to this e-mail／Please find attached our latest price list.
enclosed（附在信封裡）隨信附上的The enclosed card is for Julia.／Please find enclosed a cheque…
including介系詞：包括……；放在被包括的東西前面The price is $300, including tax.
included形容詞：（某樣東西）包括在內；放在名詞後面The price is $300, tax included.
following介系詞：在……之後；the following 是「接下來的、下列的」Following the meeting, there will be lunch.／the following morning
regarding介系詞：關於（正式）；後面不加 toI am writing regarding your order.
given介系詞：考慮到……Given the tight schedule, we should start now.

每個字再細一點：

  • enclosed：跟信一起裝在信封裡寄給某人。The enclosed card is for Julia.；正式的寫法 Please find enclosed a cheque in settlement of your invoice.出處
  • attach：有「隨信寄上」的用法，I attach (= am sending, usually with a letter) a copy of our latest report.；the attached file、Please find attached 是同理的寫法。出處
  • including：介系詞。Eight people, including two children, were injured in the explosion.出處
  • included：形容詞，只放在名詞後面。The trip cost a total of £250, insurance included.出處
  • following：不加 the 時意思是「在……之後、回應……、因為……」；the following 加名詞是「下一個」，不加名詞時用來帶出一串清單。出處
  • regarding：介系詞，意思是 about，屬於正式用法；後面從來不接 to。出處出處
  • given：當介系詞，意思是知道或考慮到某件事。Given his age, he's a remarkably fast runner.出處
  • Please find attached the agenda for Monday's meeting.（附件是週一會議的議程。）
  • Please review the enclosed brochure for details.（詳情請看隨信附上的手冊。）
  • All staff, including managers, must complete the training.（所有員工，包括主管，都要完成訓練。）
  • The room rate is $180, breakfast included.（房價 180 美元，含早餐。）
  • Following the merger, the company changed its name.（合併之後，公司改了名字。）
  • Please bring the following: your ID, a pen, and your résumé.（請帶下列物品：身分證、筆和履歷。）
  • Regarding your question about delivery, we ship within two days.（關於您詢問的出貨，我們兩天內寄出。）
  • Given the bad weather, the outdoor event will be held indoors.（考慮到天氣不好，戶外活動改在室內舉行。）

綜合，混在一起考

綜合練習：照多益第五部分的樣子

下面八題把這課的觀念混在一起。先問：這個名詞（或主詞）是自己做、還是被做？是人在感覺、還是東西讓人有感覺？

The slow Internet connection in the branch office is very ______.

  • frustrated
  • frustrating
  • frustrate
  • frustration

網路慢讓人沮喪，主詞是東西，用 -ing：frustrating。frustrated 是人覺得沮喪。

Customers ______ their orders before May 1 will receive a 10 percent discount.

  • placed
  • place
  • places
  • placing

顧客自己下訂單，主動，後面有受詞 their orders：customers placing＝customers who place。主要動詞是 will receive。

______ by more than 2,000 people, the conference was a great success.

  • Attended
  • Attending
  • Having attended
  • Attend

會議是「被」兩千多人參加的，by 帶出參加的人，被動，用過去分詞 Attended。

The sales team was very ______ with the results of the campaign.

  • pleasing
  • please
  • pleased
  • pleasure

業務團隊（人）覺得高興，後面接 with：pleased with。pleasing 是「讓人高興的」。

New employees watch a short video ______ the company's history.

  • describing
  • described
  • describe
  • describes

影片在介紹公司歷史，主動，後面有受詞：a video describing…＝a video which describes。句子的主要動詞已經是 watch，空格不能再放有時態的動詞。

______ all the applications, the committee will announce the results on Friday.

  • Reviewed
  • Having reviewed
  • Having been reviewed
  • Review

委員會先審完所有申請、再公布結果：主動、先完成，用 Having reviewed。

Please check the ______ figures and let me know if anything looks wrong.

  • revising
  • revise
  • revises
  • revised

數字是被修改過的，被動，用過去分詞 revised：修正後的數字。

______ your recent inquiry, the item you requested is now back in stock.

  • Regarding
  • Regarded
  • Regard
  • Regarding to

Regarding 是介系詞（關於），直接接名詞 your recent inquiry，不加 to。它是介系詞，所以不用跟主詞 the item 對上。

重點整理

  • 分詞當形容詞：名詞自己做這個動作用 -ing（rising costs、the remaining seats），名詞被做用過去分詞（the updated schedule、a signed contract）。
  • 情緒形容詞：-ing 是「讓人覺得」，說事情、東西（an interesting proposal、disappointing results）；-ed 是「人覺得」（interested in、satisfied with、excited about、confused by）。主詞是東西幾乎都用 -ing；人用 -ing 是「這個人讓別人覺得……」。
  • 分詞片語放名詞後面，可以看成關係子句的縮短版（名詞當主詞、受詞都行）：主動把動詞改成 -ing（the people attending the meeting、anyone wishing to join），被動拿掉 who／which＋be 留下過去分詞（the form attached to the letter）；過去做完的一次性動作不縮成 -ing。mentioned、shown 這類習慣放名詞後面。
  • 分詞構句：兩個子句主詞相同時可以縮。主動用 -ing、被動用過去分詞（Founded in 1998, …）、先完成用 having＋過去分詞、否定 not 放前面；when、while、before、after 可以留著（When reviewing…）。
  • 懸垂分詞：分詞的主詞要等於逗號後面的主詞，對不上就要改寫（Walking into the office, I heard…）。given、including、following、regarding 是介系詞，不受這條限制。
  • 信件常用：Please find attached／enclosed…、the attached file；including tax（放前面）對 tax included（放後面）；following＝after、the following＝下列的；regarding 不加 to；Given the…＝考慮到。
