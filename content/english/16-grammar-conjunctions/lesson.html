讀完這課你會

  • 分清對等、相關與從屬連接詞
  • 依意思選對連接詞
  • 分清 because 與 because of 這類組合
  • 用對 however 這類連接副詞的標點

連接兩個地位相同的東西

對等連接詞：and、but、or、so、yet、nor

連接詞（conjunction）是把字、片語或子句接起來的字。〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉講過：同一個主詞做兩個動作時，兩個有時態的動詞中間要有 and、but 這類連接詞。這課把範圍放大：兩個子句（各有自己主詞和動詞的一組字）要接成一句，也要靠連接詞（或是這課最後一段講的分號）。

第一種是對等連接詞（coordinating conjunction），連接文法上同一類的東西，例如字、片語、子句；最常見的是 and、or、but。出處

  • 一共七個：and、but、for、or、nor、so、yet，放在獨立子句開頭，把它接到前一個子句上。出處
  • 口訣 FANBOYS：照 for、and、nor、but、or、yet、so 的順序排，第一個字母連起來是 FANBOYS。出處
連接詞意思例句
and和、而且Ms. Lin finished the report and sent it to the manager.
but但是（前後意思相反）The meeting was long, but it was very useful.
or或者You can pay by credit card or by bank transfer.
so所以（後面接結果）The client approved the budget, so we can start next week.
yet但是、然而The software is expensive, yet thousands of small firms use it.
nor也不（接在否定句後面）We didn't see the factory, nor did we meet the manager.
for因為…hummus, for it is very garlicky.
  • yet：當連接詞時意思是 but 或 nevertheless，表示對比。出處
  • nor：接在否定子句後面時，nor 後面的主詞和動詞要倒過來：We didn’t get to see the castle, nor did we see the cathedral.出處 倒裝的完整說明在〈倒裝、省略與多益文法題解題順序〉那課。

四個要注意的地方

  • 逗號：and、or、but 連接兩個主要子句（能自己成句的子句）時，主詞相同通常不加逗號，主詞不同有時會加。出處 也有寫作指南要求：第二個完整子句用對等連接詞開頭時，連接詞前面要加逗號。出處 兩種說法不完全一樣，所以這個逗號不會是選擇題的考點；自己寫信時，兩個主詞不同的子句之間加逗號比較清楚。
  • 表示結果的 so 子句一定放後面：用 so 子句連接原因和結果時，so 子句一定在第二個。出處
  • 否定句裡用 or，不用 and：動詞的否定形後面用 or：I don’t like tea or coffee.出處
  • 一次只用一個連接詞：除了兩個字一組的連接詞，連接時只用一個連接詞。出處 中文說「因為……所以……」、「雖然……但是……」，英文只能挑一個：
    ✗ Because my alarm didn’t go off, so I was late. → ✓ Because my alarm didn’t go off, I was late for work.

The hotel near the airport is expensive, ______ the rooms are very small.

  • so
  • or
  • for
  • but

價錢貴、房間卻小，跟預期相反，用 but。so 會變成「因為貴所以房間小」；for 會變成「因為房間小所以貴」；or 是二選一，都不通。

The flight to Singapore was cancelled, ______ we booked a hotel near the airport.

  • so
  • or
  • nor
  • because of

班機取消是原因，訂飯店是結果，用 so。nor 要接在否定句後面而且要倒裝；because of 是介系詞，後面不能接子句。

We have not received the invoice ______ the delivery note.

  • or
  • so
  • because
  • although

否定句裡連接兩個名詞用 or：沒收到發票，也沒收到送貨單。because、although 後面要接有主詞和動詞的子句，這裡只有名詞。

The software is expensive, ______ it is used by thousands of small companies.

  • so
  • yet
  • or
  • nor

貴，卻有幾千家小公司在用，前後相反，用 yet（＝but）。so 會變成「因為貴所以很多人用」，意思不對；nor 要接在否定後面而且要倒裝。

______ the shipment was late, we offered the customer a discount.

  • So
  • But
  • Because
  • And

逗號前面那段要能掛在後面的主要子句上，只有從屬連接詞 Because 做得到。so、but、and 是對等連接詞；so 子句帶的是結果，一定放在後面，不能放句首。

下面哪一句正確？

  • Because the printer was broken, so we sent the files to a copy shop.
  • The printer was broken, we sent the files to a copy shop.
  • Because the printer was broken, we sent the files to a copy shop.
  • So we sent the files to a copy shop, the printer was broken.

Because the printer was broken, we sent… 只用一個連接詞，正確。Because…, so… 用了兩個連接詞；The printer was broken, we sent… 兩個完整句子之間只有逗號；So we sent…, the printer was broken 把 so 子句放到前面，都錯。

The manager did not approve the budget, ______ did she explain why.

  • or
  • so
  • and
  • nor

前面是否定句，後面接「也沒有解釋」，而且 did she 已經倒裝，用 nor。or、and、so 後面不倒裝；so did she 是「她也一樣」，意思也不對。

兩個字一組，前後對稱

相關連接詞：both…and、either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also、whether…or

相關連接詞（correlative conjunction）是兩個字一組、前後呼應的連接詞，有些書把它們算成對等連接詞的一種（有兩個部分的對等連接詞）。出處 前後兩半接的東西要同一類，例如字、片語、子句。出處

相關連接詞意思例句
both A and BA 和 B 兩個都Both the sales team and the design team will attend.
either A or B不是 A 就是 B，二選一You can reach us either by phone or by email.
neither A nor BA 和 B 都不Neither the hotel nor the airline offered a refund.
not only A but (also) B不只 A，還有 BThe new system is not only faster but also cheaper.
whether A or B是 A 還是 B；不管 A 還是 BWe haven't decided whether to hire a designer or train someone.
  • both…and：強調兩件事的連結，比單用 and 更強。出處
  • neither…nor：是 either…or 的否定形：Neither Joe nor Maria will be at the wedding.出處
  • not only…but also：用在正式的場合，also 有時可以省略。出處 Not only 放句首時要倒裝，在〈倒裝、省略與多益文法題解題順序〉那課講。
  • whether…or：帶出一個有兩個選項的子句，另有兩條限制：出處
  • 後面接 to＋原形時只能用 whether，不能用 if。
  • whether…or not 可以表示「不管……都」，這時也不能換成 either。

當主詞時，動詞的單複數：

  • both 帶的名詞接複數動詞，either、neither 帶的名詞接單數動詞。這條講的是 either、neither 單獨當數量詞用的時候（例如 either option、neither of the supermarkets），不是 either A or B。出處
  • either…or、neither…nor 一單一複：動詞跟最靠近的名詞一致；建議把複數放在靠近動詞的位置、用複數動詞。出處 完整說明在〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉那課。

Both the sales team ______ the marketing team will attend the training session.

  • or
  • and
  • nor
  • but

both 配 and：Both A and B。

You can contact our support center either by phone ______ by email.

  • and
  • nor
  • but
  • or

either 配 or：either by phone or by email，兩種方式選一種。

Neither the hotel ______ the airline offered us a refund.

  • nor
  • and
  • but
  • also

neither 配 nor：飯店和航空公司都沒有退款。

The new accounting system is not only faster ______ cheaper to run.

  • or
  • nor
  • but also
  • so

not only 配 but also：不只比較快，還比較省錢。

We have not decided ______ to hire a new designer or train someone in our team.

  • if
  • whether
  • either
  • both

兩個選項、後面接 to＋原形，用 whether。to＋原形前面不用 if；這種「還沒決定是哪一個」的句子也不用 either。

Both the printer and the scanner on the third floor ______ repairs.

  • needs
  • is needing
  • needing
  • need

both A and B 是兩樣東西，接複數動詞 need。needing 不是有時態的動詞。

Either Mr. Ito or his assistants ______ going to meet the client at the airport.

  • are
  • is
  • was
  • has

either A or B 一單一複時，動詞跟靠近的那個；靠近動詞的是 assistants（複數），用 are。

The outdoor reception will go ahead ______ it rains or not.

  • either
  • both
  • whether
  • neither

whether…or not＝不管下不下雨。這個意思不用 either。

一個子句掛在另一個上面

從屬連接詞與時間：when、while、before、until、as soon as、since

第二種是從屬連接詞（subordinating conjunction），常見的有 after、(al)though、as、before、if、since、that、until、when、whereas、while、once、as soon as、provided that。這些連接詞後面接子句時，這個子句就變成從屬子句：不能自己成句，要掛在主要子句上才是完整的句子。出處

有些清單也把 so 列成從屬連接詞；這課照上面「七個對等連接詞」的分法，把表示結果的 so 算成對等連接詞。分法不同，不影響選擇題。

  • 逗號：從屬子句在主要子句前面時，通常在從屬子句結尾加逗號；主要子句在前面時，不需要逗號。出處
  • 位置固定在子句開頭：從屬連接詞一定在子句的開頭，整個從屬子句可以搬到主要子句前面或後面。出處

時間連接詞

when、after、before、until、since、while、once、as、as soon as 都是從屬連接詞，把一個動作或事件連到一個時間點。出處

連接詞意思例句
when……的時候When the client arrives, please call me.
while在……的期間（同時進行）The phone rang while we were having lunch.
before／after在……之前／之後Please check the figures before you send the report.
until一直到……Please wait here until Ms. Chen comes to meet you.
as soon as／once一……就……We will ship the order as soon as we receive payment.
since自從……以來Mr. Park has worked in Tokyo since the office opened.
  • while：表示動作或事件同時發生，過去、現在、未來都可以用。出處
  • since＋主要子句用現在完成式：since 指過去某個時間點時，since 後面可以用過去簡單式或現在完成式，主要子句用現在完成式：They haven’t received any junk mail since they moved house.出處
  • 講未來不用 will：指未來時間的連接詞後面不用 will：
    ✗ I will call you as soon as I will get to the office. → ✓ I will call you as soon as I get to the office.出處 完整說明在〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉那課。

Please wait in the lobby ______ Ms. Chen comes down to meet you.

  • during
  • until
  • despite
  • because of

空格後面是子句（Ms. Chen comes），要連接詞；「等到陳小姐下來」用 until。during、despite、because of 是介系詞，後面不能接子句。

I will call you ______ the shipment arrives at the warehouse.

  • during
  • since
  • despite
  • as soon as

「貨一到就打給你」用 as soon as，子句用現在式講未來。since 當「自從」講要配現在完成式，跟 will call 接不上，當「因為」講意思也不通；during、despite 後面要接名詞。

Mr. Park has worked in the Tokyo office ______ it opened in 2019.

  • since
  • when
  • until
  • while

主要子句是現在完成式 has worked，從 2019 年一路算到現在，用 since。when、while 不跟「一路到現在」的現在完成式搭；until 是「一直到」，意思反了。

The phone rang ______ we were having lunch in the cafeteria.

  • during
  • while
  • despite
  • because of

吃午餐那段時間裡電話響了，後面是子句，用 while。during 也是「在……期間」，但它是介系詞，後面只能接名詞。

We will start the meeting as soon as everyone ______.

  • will arrive
  • arrived
  • arrives
  • arriving

as soon as 子句講未來，用現在式；everyone 接單數，用 arrives。will 只放主要子句。

Ms. Lee had already left the office ______ the client called, so she missed the call.

  • after
  • until
  • before
  • since

她錯過電話，表示客戶打來時她已經走了：離開在前、來電在後，用 before。after 會變成來電之後才走，那就不會錯過。

為什麼，與「雖然」

原因與讓步：because、since、as；although、even though、whereas

表示原因：because、since、as

as、because、since 都帶出從屬子句，把結果和原因連起來。三個意思很接近，差別在語氣：出處

  • because：比 as 和 since 常見。
  • as、since：比 because 正式。

三個意思一樣，練習題要分的是它們跟其他連接詞的不同。

  • since 有兩個意思：「自從」（時間）和「因為」（原因）。當「因為」用時，since 帶出從屬子句說明原因：They couldn’t deliver the parcel since no one was there to answer the door.出處
  • because 子句不能自己成句：口語可以單獨回答 Because…，但正式寫作不單獨使用 because 子句。出處

表示讓步：although、though、even though

讓步是「雖然 A，還是 B」：A 本來會讓人以為 B 不會發生。

  • although、though：都是「儘管」的意思，都是從屬連接詞。出處
  • even though：比 although 稍微強一點、更強調。出處

兩個要注意的地方：

  • although 和 but 不能一起用：✗ But I’m tall, I’m not as tall as Bill. → ✓ Although I’m tall, I’m not as tall as Bill.出處 前面說過，一次只用一個連接詞，所以也不說 Although…, but…。
  • even if＝不管是不是：even if 的意思是 whether or not：Even if you run, you’ll still be late.出處

對比兩件事：whereas、while

  • whereas：表示兩件事實或想法之間的對比。在對比的句子裡跟 while 一樣，但 while 指時間時不能換成 whereas。出處
  • while：最常用來講時間，但也可以表示「儘管」（＝although）。出處

例句：

  • Because the train was delayed, Mr. Ota was late for the interview.（因為火車誤點，太田先生面試遲到了。）
  • As everyone already knows each other, we can skip the introductions.（大家都認識了，自我介紹就省略。）
  • Although the budget was small, the campaign was a success.（雖然預算不多，宣傳活動很成功。）
  • The project was finished on time even though two members were on leave.（雖然有兩個人在休假，專案還是準時完成。）
  • Sales in Asia rose, whereas sales in Europe fell slightly.（亞洲的銷售成長，歐洲則小幅下滑。）

______ the train was delayed, Mr. Ota arrived late for his interview.

  • Although
  • Because
  • Despite
  • Unless

火車誤點是遲到的原因，用 Because。Although 會變成「雖然誤點，還是遲到」，前後不相反；Despite 是介系詞，後面不能接子句；Unless 是「除非」。

______ the meeting room was small, everyone managed to find a seat.

  • Because
  • Since
  • Due to
  • Although

房間小，本來以為坐不下，結果大家都有位子，用讓步的 Although。Because、Since 會變成「因為房間小，所以都有位子」；Due to 是介系詞，後面不能接子句。

The project was completed on time ______ two team members were on leave.

  • even though
  • because
  • so
  • despite

有人休假，本來可能延誤，結果還是準時，用 even though。because 的因果接不上；so 會把「有人休假」變成準時的結果；despite 後面要接名詞。

We will finish the report today ______ we have to stay late.

  • so that
  • even if
  • whereas
  • despite

不管要不要加班，今天都會寫完，用 even if（＝whether or not）。so that 是「為了」，whereas 是對比兩件事，despite 後面要接名詞。

Sales in Asia rose by 10 percent, ______ sales in Europe fell slightly.

  • because
  • so that
  • unless
  • whereas

把亞洲和歐洲兩件事對比，用 whereas。because、so that、unless 的意思都接不上。

Could you answer my phone ______ I am at the dentist this afternoon?

  • whereas
  • during
  • while
  • despite

「我去看牙醫的那段時間」是時間，用 while。while 指時間時不能換成 whereas；during、despite 後面要接名詞。

下面哪一句正確？

  • Although the hotel was expensive, but the service was excellent.
  • Although the hotel was expensive, the service was excellent.
  • But the hotel was expensive, the service was excellent.
  • Despite the hotel was expensive, the service was excellent.

Although the hotel was expensive, the service was excellent. 只有一個連接詞，正確。Although…, but… 用了兩個；But the hotel was expensive, … 的 but 不能帶出掛在主要子句上的從屬子句；Despite 後面要接名詞，不能接子句。

______ everyone here already knows each other, we can skip the introductions.

  • Although
  • Unless
  • Despite
  • As

大家都認識是省略介紹的原因，用 As（＝because）。Although 意思相反；Unless 是「除非」；Despite 後面要接名詞。

如果、為了、結果

條件、目的與結果：unless、as long as、in case；so that；so…that、such…that

條件

連接詞意思例句
if如果If you have any questions, please call me.
unless除非（＝if…not）We cannot ship the order unless we receive payment.
provided (that)、as long as只要（附帶條件）You may work from home as long as you finish your tasks on time.
in case以防萬一Take some extra brochures in case more visitors come.
  • unless：意思是「除非」，跟 if…not 一樣。它跟 if 一樣是條件詞，所以 unless 子句裡不用 will 或 would。出處
  • as long as、provided that：用來加上特定的條件或設限制，例子有 as long as、provided (that)、on condition that；provided (that) 比較正式、多用在書面。出處
  • in case 不是 if：in case 講的是為了可能發生的情況先做準備，不當 if 用。另外 in case of 是介系詞，後面接名詞：In case of breakdown, please press the alarm button…出處

目的：so that

  • so that 和 in order that 用來談目的，常和 can、would、will 這類助動詞一起用。
  • so that 比 in order that 常見得多，in order that 比較正式。出處
  • so that 後面接子句；in order to 後面接原形動詞：You have to look at the picture really carefully in order to see all the detail.出處

結果：so＋形容詞／副詞＋that，such＋名詞片語＋that

such 與 so 的分工：

  • 接什麼：such 接名詞片語，so 接形容詞或副詞；much、many、little、few 前面用 so，不用 such。出處
  • 加 that 子句：後面加 that 子句說出結果，這個句型裡不用 very：It was so hot that we didn’t leave the air-conditioned room all day.出處
  • 例句：He was such a good teacher that we all passed the exam.、There's so much noise that I can't think!出處

so＋形容詞（so busy）；such＋a＋形容詞＋名詞（such a busy week）；so many／so much＋名詞。

We cannot ship your order ______ we receive full payment.

  • although
  • unless
  • whereas
  • despite

「除非收到全額付款，否則不能出貨」用 unless。although、whereas 意思接不上；despite 後面要接名詞。

You may work from home on Fridays ______ you finish your tasks on time.

  • unless
  • in case of
  • as long as
  • despite

「只要準時完成工作」是附帶條件，用 as long as。unless 會變成「可以在家工作，除非你準時完成」——準時完成反而不能在家，意思反了；in case of、despite 是介系詞，後面不能接子句。

Take a few extra brochures to the trade fair ______ more visitors come than we expect.

  • in case
  • unless
  • in case of
  • despite

多帶一些型錄，是為了訪客比預期多時有得發，用 in case。in case of、despite 是介系詞，後面接名詞；unless 是「除非」，意思不通。

______ fire, do not use the elevators.

  • In case
  • Unless
  • Although
  • In case of

空格後面只有名詞 fire，要介系詞 In case of（萬一發生）。In case、Unless、Although 是連接詞，後面要接子句。

Please arrive ten minutes early ______ we can start the meeting on time.

  • in order to
  • so that
  • because of
  • despite

空格後面是子句 we can start…，說明目的，用 so that。in order to 後面要接原形動詞；because of、despite 後面接名詞。

Ms. Kim left the office early ______ catch the last train to Busan.

  • in order to
  • so that
  • because
  • although

空格後面是原形動詞 catch，用 in order to。so that、because、although 後面都要接有主詞的子句。

Demand for the new model was ______ high that the factory added a night shift.

  • such
  • too
  • very
  • so

後面是子句，還是名詞

連接詞對介系詞：because 對 because of、although 對 despite、while 對 during

中文都翻成「因為」「雖然」「在……期間」，英文卻分兩種：連接詞後面接子句（有主詞、有時態的動詞），介系詞後面接名詞、代名詞或 -ing。介系詞本身的用法在〈介系詞〉那課。

意思連接詞＋子句介系詞＋名詞或 -ing
因為because、since、asbecause of、due to、owing to
雖然、儘管although、though、even thoughdespite、in spite of
在……期間whileduring
萬一in casein case of

每一列的用法：

  • because 對 because of：because 是連接詞，用在有動詞的子句前面；because of 是介系詞，一定用在名詞或名詞片語前面：I was late because there was heavy traffic.、I was late because of heavy traffic.出處 due to 和 owing to 也是 because of 的意思：The bus was delayed due to heavy snow.出處、The concert has been cancelled owing to lack of interest.出處
  • although 對 despite：in spite of 和 despite 後面接名詞、動名詞（動詞的 -ing 形）或代名詞；although 和 even though 後面接主詞和動詞。出處 despite 後面永遠不加 of（✗ despite of），in spite of 是分開的三個字，也不直接接 that 子句，要接子句就寫 in spite of the fact that、despite the fact that。出處
  • while 對 during：during 是介系詞，不是連接詞，所以後面一定接名詞或代名詞：
    ✗ She studies during she is on the train. → ✓ She studies while she is on the train.出處
  • in case 對 in case of：in case 是連接詞或副詞，in case of 是介系詞。出處

看空格後面：有主詞＋有時態的動詞 → 連接詞；只有名詞片語 → 介系詞。

-ing 要多看一眼：despite、in spite of 後面可以接 -ing（They arrived late despite leaving in plenty of time.）出處，但 although、though 在正式寫作也能接 -ing 或省略動詞的子句（Though more expensive, the new model is safer and more efficient.）出處，while、when 接 -ing 的寫法在〈分詞與分詞構句〉講過。所以空格後面是 -ing 時，不能只靠「名詞還是子句」判斷，要再看意思和其他選項。

  • The reception was moved indoors because of the heavy rain.／…because it was raining heavily.（因為下大雨，酒會移到室內。）
  • Despite the high price, the new model sold well.／Although the price was high, …（雖然價格高，新機型還是賣得很好。）
  • Please keep your phone on silent during the presentation.／…while Mr. Lee is presenting.（簡報時請把手機調成靜音。）
  • The shipment was delayed due to a problem at the port.（因為港口出問題，貨晚到了。）
  • Despite the fact that sales fell, the company opened two new stores.（雖然業績下滑，公司還是開了兩家新店。）

看起來像連接詞的副詞

連接副詞：however、therefore、moreover，以及標點

連接副詞是 however、therefore、moreover 這類「把兩句的意思接起來，但文法上是副詞」的字。意思上跟連接詞類似，文法上不一樣：出處

  • 連接副詞（however、therefore、as a result 這類）：是副詞片語，可以放在副詞能放的任何位置。
  • 從屬連接詞：不能隨便換位置，而且它在兩個子句之間建立文法上的連結。
意思連接副詞例句
但是、不過howeverThis is one possible solution to the problem. However, there are others.出處
儘管如此neverthelessNevertheless, accidents still occur.出處
因此therefore、consequentlyThe company is trying to reduce its costs; consequently, staff who leave are not being replaced.出處
此外、而且moreover、furthermore、in additionThe whole report is badly written. Moreover, it's inaccurate.出處
不然、否則otherwiseI'd better write it down, otherwise I'll forget it.出處

標點：三種寫法

  • 句號後面開新句：The supplier raised its prices. However, the quality is still good.
  • 分號後面接：第二個完整子句用 however 這類字開頭時，前面要用分號：Jim studied in the Sweet Shop for his chemistry quiz; however, it was hard to concentrate because of the noise. 這類字常見的有 also、consequently、furthermore、however、moreover、nevertheless、therefore。出處 分號也可以代替句號，把兩個主要子句分開。出處
  • 放在句子中間：前後各一個逗號：There may, however, be other reasons that we don't know about.出處；也可以放在 and 後面：We were unable to get funding and therefore had to abandon the project.。出處

不能像連接詞一樣，只用逗號接兩個句子

兩個完整句子之間只放一個逗號，英文叫 comma splice，是錯句。出處 改法有三種：出處

  • 換成句號：✗ The price is high, the quality is good. → ✓ The price is high. The quality is good.
  • 換成分號：The price is high; the quality is good.
  • 加連接詞：The price is high, but the quality is good.

however、therefore 是副詞，不是連接詞：

  • 只用逗號接兩個句子，一樣是 comma splice：✗ The price is high, however the quality is good.
  • 也不能像 but 那樣連接字和片語：✗ My teacher is very nice however a bit strict. → ✓ My teacher is very nice but a bit strict.出處
要說的意思連接詞（逗號就夠）連接副詞（句號或分號）
但是The price is high, but the quality is good.The price is high; however, the quality is good.
所以The flight was cancelled, so we stayed another night.The flight was cancelled. Therefore, we stayed another night.
  • otherwise 例外：字典也把 otherwise 標成連接詞，所以逗號後面可以直接接：I'd better write it down, otherwise I'll forget it.出處
  • in addition 與 in addition to：in addition 是連接副詞；in addition to 是介系詞，後面接名詞或 -ing：In addition to his apartment in Manhattan, he has a villa in Italy…、All employees receive paid holiday and sick leave. In addition, we offer a range of benefits for new parents.出處

The proposal is very well written. ______, it is far too expensive for our budget.

  • Therefore
  • However
  • Moreover
  • Because

寫得好、但太貴，前後相反，用 However。Therefore 是「因此」；Moreover 是同方向再加一點；Because 子句不能單獨成句。

漲價是原因，找新供應商是結果，用 Therefore。Although 是從屬連接詞，後面那段不能自己成句；However、Otherwise 意思不對。

重點整理

  • 對等連接詞（for、and、nor、but、or、yet、so，口訣 FANBOYS）連接同一類的東西；表示結果的 so 子句一定放後面；否定句裡用 or；一句話只用一個連接詞，不說 Because…, so…、Although…, but…。
  • 相關連接詞（correlative conjunction）：both…and、either…or、neither…nor、not only…but also、whether…or；前後接同一類的東西。whether 後面可以接 to＋原形，if 不行。
  • 從屬連接詞帶出的子句不能自己成句，要掛在主要子句上；從屬子句放前面時加逗號。時間：when、while、before、after、until、as soon as、once、since；講未來時，子句用現在式。
  • 原因 because、since、as；讓步 although、though、even though（even if＝不管是不是）；對比 whereas、while（while 指時間時不能換成 whereas）；條件 unless（＝if…not）、as long as、provided that、in case；目的 so that＋子句、in order to＋原形；結果 so＋形容詞＋that、such＋名詞片語＋that、so many／much。
  • 看空格後面：子句用連接詞（because、although、while、in case），名詞用介系詞（because of、due to、despite、in spite of、during、in case of）；despite 不加 of，要接子句就用 despite the fact that。
  • however、therefore、moreover、nevertheless、consequently 是連接副詞：用句號或分號隔開，後面加逗號，或用兩個逗號夾在句中；只用逗號接兩個句子是錯句（comma splice），要換成 but、so 這類連接詞。
