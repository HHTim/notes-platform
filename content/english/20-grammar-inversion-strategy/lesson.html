  • 用一套做題順序處理多益閱讀第五、六部分：先看四個選項是哪一型，再到空格前後找線索，並知道每一型該回去複習哪一課

先看懂「倒裝」在做什麼

倒裝：把助動詞搬到主詞前面

倒裝（inversion）就是把句子平常的語序倒過來，最常見的是把主詞和動詞的順序對調。劍橋：「倒裝是把一個結構平常的語序反過來，最常見的是主詞與動詞的語序」（Inversion happens when we reverse (invert) the normal word order of a structure, most commonly the subject-verb word order.）。最熟悉的倒裝其實就是問句：She sings. 變成 Does she sing?，They are working. 變成 Are they working?。出處

這課要講的倒裝，都是直述句長得像問句：意思不是在問，只是某個字放到句首，後面的主詞和助動詞就照問句的順序排。做法跟問句一模一樣：

  • 句子裡已經有助動詞（have、will、can、should……）或 be 動詞：把它搬到主詞前面。We have never seen… → Never have we seen…。
  • 句子裡沒有助動詞：照問句的規矩補 do／does／did，主要動詞改回原形。英國文化協會：「如果沒有助動詞，就要加一個，例如現在簡單式加 do、過去簡單式加 did」（If there is no auxiliary verb, we need to add one. For example, we add do for present simple verbs and did for past simple verbs.），例子 Rarely do we find such talent.（＝We rarely find such talent.）。出處

倒裝＝句首的字＋助動詞（或 be）＋主詞＋其餘部分。沒有助動詞就補 do／does／did，動詞回原形。

這種倒裝用在正式的場合。英國文化協會說倒裝用來加強語氣，「尤其是在正式的英文裡」（We can use inversion to add emphasis, especially in formal English.）。出處 公司公告、正式書信、致詞稿都看得到；平常講話照一般語序就好。

Never、Rarely、Not only、Hardly、No sooner、Only after、Under no circumstances

否定副詞放句首：Never have we…、Not only did…

劍橋：「在正式的文體裡，把帶否定意思的副詞（例如 never、seldom、rarely、scarcely、hardly）放在句首來強調時，主詞和助動詞或情態助動詞要倒裝」（In formal styles, when we use an adverb with negative meaning (e.g. never, seldom, rarely, scarcely, hardly) in front position for emphasis, we invert the subject (s) and auxiliary (aux)/modal verb），例子 Seldom does one hear a politician say ‘sorry’.（或 One seldom hears …）。出處 英國文化協會把會引起倒裝的開頭整理成幾組，下表照它的分法排：出處

開頭意思職場例句
Never、Rarely、Seldom從來沒有、很少Never have we received so many orders in one week.
Rarely does the CEO attend team meetings.
Not only…but (also)不只……還……Not only did the team finish early, but it also stayed under budget.
Hardly／Scarcely…when一……就……（過去）Hardly had the meeting started when the power went out.
No sooner…than一……就……（過去）No sooner had we launched the website than orders started coming in.
Only after／Only when／Only then／Only later直到……才Only after the audit did we find the error.
Under no circumstances、At no time、In no way、On no account、Not until絕不、從來沒有、直到……才Under no circumstances should staff share their passwords.
Not until Friday did the client reply.
Little幾乎不、完全沒Little did we know that the deal would fall through.

每一組的根據與要注意的地方：

  • Never、rarely、seldom：英國文化協會說這幾個「常跟現在完成式、過去完成式，或 can、could 這類情態助動詞一起用」（These adverbials are often used with present perfect, past perfect or modals like can and could.），例子 Seldom has she taken a day off work.。出處
  • Not only：劍橋：「為了加強語氣，可以把 not only 放在子句開頭，這時主詞和動詞要倒裝」（To add emphasis, we can use not only at the beginning of a clause. When we do this, we invert the subject and the verb），沒有助動詞或 be 動詞時用 do、does、did：Not only did she forget my birthday, but she also didn’t even apologise for forgetting it.。出處 倒裝只發生在 Not only 那一半，but 後面照一般語序。not only…but also 不放句首的用法在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉講過。
  • Hardly…when、No sooner…than：都是「一件事一發生，另一件馬上接著發生」。英國文化協會：這幾個「通常配過去完成式；hardly、scarcely、barely 後面那半句用 when，no sooner 用 than」（are usually used with past perfect. Hardly, scarcely and barely are followed by when in the contrasting clause, and no sooner is followed by than.）。出處 劍橋的例子：Hardly had I arrived there when I was called back to the head office 100 miles away.出處、No sooner had they started their walk than it started to rain.出處。先發生的那件用過去完成式（倒裝在這半句），接著發生的那件用過去簡單式；過去完成式在〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉。
  • Only＋時間：英國文化協會列出 only after、only if／when、only then、only later，例子 Only when they refilled my glass did I realise it was broken.。出處 注意倒裝的位置：Only when 後面那個子句（they refilled my glass）照一般語序，倒裝的是後面的主要子句（did I realise）。
  • no、not 開頭的片語：英國文化協會列出 under no circumstances、on no account、at no time、in no way、on no condition、not until、not only，例子 Under no circumstances should children travel without an adult.。出處 劍橋也說 not 加介系詞片語或子句放句首時要倒裝（We also invert the subject and verb after not + a prepositional phrase or a clause in initial position），例子 Not till I got home did I realise my wallet was missing.。出處
  • Little：英國文化協會：這種句子裡的 little「是否定或限制的意思」（little has a negative or restrictive meaning），例子 Little did she know then that she would become the company director.（＝She didn't know then…）。出處

不倒裝的 only：only 只修飾主詞時，主詞本身就在句首，語序照常。劍橋：「如果焦點是主詞，就把 only 放在句首」（If the subject is the focus, we put only in front position），例子 Only Jason knows where the key is kept.，語序沒有變。出處 所以看到 Only 開頭先分辨：Only 後面直接是主詞（Only the manager can approve refunds.）不倒裝；Only 後面是時間、條件、副詞（Only after approval、Only then）才倒裝。

更多職場例句：

  • Never has our company faced such strong competition.（我們公司從沒遇過這麼強的競爭。）
  • Seldom do our drivers work on weekends.（我們的司機很少週末上班。）沒有助動詞，補 do，work 用原形。
  • Not only does the new model use less power, but it is also quieter.（新機型不只比較省電，也比較安靜。）
  • Scarcely had the guests arrived when the fire alarm went off.（客人才剛到，火警警報就響了。）
  • Only then did the manager realize the mistake.（經理到那時才發現錯誤。）
  • At no time was customer data at risk.（客戶資料從頭到尾都沒有外洩的風險。）be 動詞直接搬到前面。
  • On no account should this door be left unlocked.（這扇門絕對不可以不上鎖。）

Never ______ such a strong response to a product launch.

  • have we seen
  • we have seen
  • we have never seen
  • have we saw

Never 放句首要倒裝：助動詞 have 搬到主詞 we 前面，後面接過去分詞 seen。we have seen 沒倒裝；we have never seen 跟句首的 Never 重複否定；have 後面要過去分詞 seen，不是過去式 saw。

Rarely ______ late for a client meeting.

  • Mr. Lin is
  • does Mr. Lin
  • is Mr. Lin
  • Mr. Lin does

原句是 Mr. Lin is rarely late，有 be 動詞，倒裝時直接把 is 搬到主詞前面：Rarely is Mr. Lin late。Mr. Lin is 沒倒裝；does 後面要接原形動詞，late 是形容詞，接不上。

Seldom ______ our drivers work on Sundays.

  • are
  • have
  • does
  • do

原句 Our drivers seldom work…，沒有助動詞，倒裝要補 do；主詞 our drivers 是複數，用 do，後面的 work 保持原形。are、have 後面不能接原形 work；does 配單數主詞。

Not only ______ the deadline, but the team also cut costs by 10 percent.

  • the team met
  • did the team meet
  • met the team
  • the team did meet

Not only 放句首要倒裝；原句是過去簡單式 met、沒有助動詞，補 did，動詞回原形：did the team meet。the team met、the team did meet 都沒倒裝；met the team 把主要動詞搬到前面，一般動詞不能這樣倒裝。

Hardly ______ the presentation when the projector stopped working.

  • she had started
  • has she started
  • she started
  • had she started

Hardly…when 是「才剛……就……」，先發生的那件用過去完成式，而且 Hardly 放句首要倒裝：Hardly had she started…when…。she had started、she started 沒倒裝；has she started 是現在完成式，對不上後面的過去式 stopped。

No sooner had the contract been signed ______ the supplier raised its prices.

  • as
  • than
  • then
  • that

No sooner 配 than：「合約才剛簽好，供應商就漲價了」。then 是副詞，不能連接兩個子句；as、that 不跟 no sooner 搭配。

Only after the safety inspection ______ the new production line.

  • we will open
  • we are opening
  • will we open
  • opening

Only after＋名詞放句首，後面的主要子句要倒裝：will we open（＝We will open the line only after the inspection）。we will open、we are opening 都沒倒裝；opening 不是有時態的動詞，句子會沒有動詞。

Only when the client called ______ that the invoice had been sent to the wrong address.

  • we realized
  • we did realize
  • had we realize
  • did we realize

Only when 帶的子句（the client called）照一般語序，倒裝的是後面的主要子句：did we realize（原句過去式 realized，補 did、動詞回原形）。we realized、we did realize 沒倒裝；had 後面要過去分詞 realized，不是原形。

Under no circumstances ______ share their passwords with anyone.

  • employees should
  • should employees
  • employees must
  • must employees not

Under no circumstances 放句首要倒裝：should employees share。employees should、employees must 沒倒裝；Under no circumstances 已經是否定，must employees not 再加 not 變成「任何情況下都不可以不分享」，意思反了。

下面哪一句的語序正確？

  • Only after approval refunds can be issued.
  • Only the manager can approve refunds.
  • Only then the refund was issued.
  • Only when the form is signed the refund will be issued.

Only the manager 是 only 修飾主詞，主詞本來就在句首，不倒裝，正確。其他三句的 only 帶的是時間或條件（after approval、then、when the form is signed），後面的主要子句都要倒裝：can refunds be issued、was the refund issued、will the refund be issued。

Not until the auditors arrived ______ the missing files.

  • we found
  • we did find
  • did we find
  • had we find

Not until＋子句放句首，後面的主要子句倒裝：did we find（＝We didn't find the files until the auditors arrived）。we found、we did find 沒倒裝；had 後面要過去分詞 found。

書信常見，省略 if 的倒裝

Should you…、Were I…、Had we known…：整理與指路

條件句也能拿掉 if、改用倒裝，意思不變、語氣更正式。英國文化協會：「條件句也可以不用 if、改用倒裝來寫。倒裝就是把句子平常的主詞—動詞語序倒過來。這會讓句子更正式」（Conditional sentences can also be created without if, using inversion. Inversion means reversing (inverting) the normal subject–verb word order in a sentence. This makes the sentence more formal.）。出處 完整的講解與練習在〈假設語氣〉，這裡只整理成一張表，方便跟本課其他倒裝放在一起看：

條件句if 版倒裝版怎麼改
第一類（真的有可能）If you have any questions, …Should you have any questions, …if 換成 Should，動詞用原形
第二類（跟現在事實相反，或未來不太可能）If I were in your position, …
If prices rose, …		Were I in your position, …
Were prices to rise, …		be 動詞直接換成 Were；其他動詞改成 Were＋主詞＋to＋原形
第三類（跟過去事實相反）If we had known, …Had we known, …拿掉 if，Had 搬到主詞前面
否定If I hadn't been busy, …Had I not been busy, …not 放在主詞後面，不寫成 Hadn't I

表的根據：英國文化協會說第一類「把 if 換成助動詞 should，動詞改成原形」（we replace if with the auxiliary verb should and change the verb into the infinitive），例子 Should a customer wish to cancel their booking, they should notify us within seven days.；第二類「把 if 換成 were，動詞改成 to＋原形」（we replace if with were and change the verb form to to + infinitive），原本是 be 動詞時「直接把 be 換成 were 再倒裝」（We just replace the existing form of be with were, and invert the subject and verb.）；第三類「拿掉 if，把主詞和助動詞 had 倒過來」（we remove if and invert the subject and the auxiliary verb had）；否定「把 not 放在主詞後面」（we put not after the subject），例子 Had I not been busy this morning, I would have attended the meeting.。出處 劍橋也說在正式的場合可以用 should＋主詞＋動詞代替 if：Should you wish to cancel your order, please contact our customer service department…。出處

做題時的線索：句首是 Should、Were、Had，而且後面跟著逗號和另一個子句，多半是省略 if 的倒裝。三個字怎麼選，看主詞後面接什麼：原形 → Should；過去分詞 → Had；to 不定詞，或者主詞後面沒有動詞（直接接名詞、形容詞、in your position 這類片語）→ Were。最後再看主要子句是 will（或祈使句）、would＋原形，還是 would have＋過去分詞，對回表中的三類。

Should the shipment ______ late, we will notify you immediately.

  • arrives
  • arrived
  • arrive
  • arriving

Should 開頭的倒裝（＝If the shipment should arrive late），should 是助動詞，後面接原形 arrive。arrives、arrived、arriving 都不能接在助動詞後面。

______ the board approved the merger, the two companies would have combined last year.

  • Had
  • If
  • Should
  • Were

主要子句是 would have combined last year，跟過去事實相反，條件要用 had＋過去分詞；拿掉 if 倒裝成 Had the board approved。If the board approved 是第二類的形式，對不上 would have；Should 後面要原形；Were 開頭的倒裝要寫成 Were＋主詞＋to 原形（Were the board to approve），而且是第二類，對不上 would have。

Were our office closer to the airport, it ______ easier for clients to visit us.

  • will be
  • would be
  • is
  • has been

Were our office closer＝If our office were closer（辦公室其實不近，跟現在事實相反），主要子句用 would＋原形：would be。will be、is、has been 都是講真的情況。

哪一句的意思等於 If you need any help, please call me.？

  • Should you needed any help, please call me.
  • Had you needed any help, please call me.
  • Were you need any help, please call me.
  • Should you need any help, please call me.

第一類的倒裝：Should＋主詞＋原形 need。Should 後面不能接過去式 needed；Had you needed 是第三類（過去的事），對不上請對方現在打電話；Were 後面要接 to 不定詞（Were you to need）。

So do I、Neither does she；Here comes、Attached is

附和句與放在句首的地點、分詞

So／Neither／Nor＋助動詞＋主詞：「……也是」「……也不」

前面講了一件事，要說「另一個人也一樣」，用 so（肯定）或 neither／nor（否定），後面倒裝：助動詞放前面、主詞放後面，主要動詞不重複。劍橋：「用 so 這樣說的時候，動詞和主詞要倒裝，主要動詞不重複」（When we use so in this way, we invert the verb and subject, and we do not repeat the main verb），例子 Geoff is a very good long-distance runner and so is his wife.、They all joined the new gym and after three weeks so did he.。出處 否定那一邊：「用 neither 和 nor＋助動詞／情態助動詞＋主詞表示『也不』」（We use neither and nor + auxiliary/modal verb + subject to mean ‘also not’），例子 Neither can I.（不說 I can’t also.）、Nor does Gina.。出處

助動詞要跟前面那句對上：前面是 be 就用 be、是 can 就用 can、前面是一般動詞的過去式就用 did、現在完成式就用 has／have。不倒裝的說法是 not … either：劍橋說「可以用 not … either 表示『也不』，但助動詞和主詞的語序不變」（We can use not … either to mean ‘also not’, but we do not change the word order of the auxiliary or modal verb and subject），例子 I haven’t either.（或 Neither have I.／Nor have I.）。出處 nor 接在否定句後面連接兩個子句、後面要倒裝，在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉講過（We didn’t see the factory, nor did we meet the manager.）。

  • Our team met its sales target, and so did the Osaka team.（我們團隊達標了，大阪團隊也是。）met 是過去式 → did。
  • Ms. Ho is attending the trade fair, and so is her manager.（何小姐會去參展，她的經理也會。）
  • The printer on the third floor isn't working, and neither is the copier.（三樓的印表機壞了，影印機也壞了。）
  • We haven't received the samples. — Neither have we.（我們還沒收到樣品。——我們也還沒。）
  • I can't join the call at 3 p.m. — I can't either.（我三點不能參加通話。——我也不能。）不倒裝的說法。

Here comes…、There is…：地點副詞放句首

劍橋：here 與 there（當地點副詞時）放句首可以倒裝，而且「可以直接用主要動詞，不需要助動詞」（After here and there, we can use a main verb without an auxiliary verb or modal verb），例子 Here comes the bus!、Here’s your coffee.。出處 但「主詞是代名詞時，主詞和動詞不倒裝」（When the subject is a pronoun, we do not invert the subject and verb），例子 There it is, on the sink.（不說 There is it）。出處

  • Here comes the airport shuttle.（機場接駁車來了。）主詞是名詞，倒裝。
  • Here it comes.（它來了。）主詞是代名詞 it，不倒裝。
  • Here are the reports you asked for.（這是你要的報告。）主詞 the reports 是複數，用 are。

Attached is…、Enclosed please find…：書信裡把分詞放句首

書信裡常把 attached（附在電子郵件裡的）、enclosed（隨信裝在信封裡的）放到句首，後面接 be 動詞再接主詞，例如 Attached is the agenda for Monday's meeting.（附件是週一會議的議程。）它就是 The agenda for Monday's meeting is attached. 把 attached 搬到前面，主詞還是後面的 the agenda，所以 be 動詞要跟後面的名詞一致：Attached are the minutes and the updated schedule.。劍橋辭典 herewith 條目收的實際用例就是這個語序：Enclosed herewith is a letter for him.。出處 另一種常見的寫法是 Please find enclosed…／Please find attached…：劍橋辭典 enclose 條目的例句 Please find enclosed an application form and information about the company.。出處 Please find attached／enclosed 在〈分詞與分詞構句〉的信件用語也整理過。

So／Neither／Nor＋助動詞＋主詞（助動詞跟前一句對上）；Here comes＋名詞，但 Here it comes；Attached is／are＋主詞（be 動詞跟後面的主詞一致）。

The sales team exceeded its target last quarter, and so ______ the marketing team.

  • was
  • has
  • did
  • does

前一句是過去簡單式 exceeded、沒有助動詞，附和句用 did：so did the marketing team。was 對不上一般動詞；has 是現在完成式、does 是現在式，都跟 last quarter 的過去式對不上。

A: I can't attend Friday's workshop. B: ______

  • Neither can I.
  • So can I.
  • Neither I can.
  • I can't too.

對方說「不能」，我也不能：Neither can I（neither＋助動詞＋主詞，倒裝）。So can I 是「我也能」，意思相反；Neither I can 沒倒裝；否定句的「也」不用 too，要用 I can't either。

Our branch hasn't received the new laptops, and ______ the Taipei office.

  • neither does
  • so has
  • either has
  • neither has

前一句是否定的現在完成式 hasn't received，附和「也還沒有」用 neither＋has＋主詞。neither does 助動詞對不上；so has 是「也收到了」，意思相反；either 不能這樣放句首倒裝。

Mr. Ito doesn't like the new logo, and I don't ______.

  • neither
  • either
  • too
  • so

不倒裝的「也不」是 not … either：I don't either。I don't neither 是雙重否定；too 用在肯定句；so 要放句首倒裝（So do I），而且是肯定的意思。

Here ______ the airport shuttle — let's get our bags.

  • come
  • coming
  • is come
  • comes

Here 放句首、主詞是名詞 the airport shuttle，倒裝成 Here comes the airport shuttle；主詞單數，動詞加 -s。coming 不是有時態的動詞；is come 不是正確的動詞形式。

A: Where's the visitor badge? B: Here ______, on the reception desk.

  • is it
  • it is
  • are they
  • its

主詞是代名詞 it 時不倒裝：Here it is。is it 是倒裝，代名詞不這樣用；badge 是單數，不用 they；its 是所有格，句子會沒有動詞。

Attached ______ the minutes of yesterday's meeting and the updated schedule.

  • are
  • is
  • be
  • being

原句是 The minutes … and the updated schedule are attached，主詞是兩樣東西（A and B），用複數 are；attached 搬到句首，主詞不變。is 對不上複數主詞；be、being 不是有時態的動詞。

When asked、If necessary、than expected、I'd like to

省略：意思很清楚時，重複的字可以拿掉

省略（ellipsis）是把照文法規矩本來應該出現、但意思已經很清楚的字拿掉。劍橋：「省略是把照文法規則通常會用到的東西拿掉（也就是不用）」（Ellipsis happens when we leave out (in other words, when we don’t use) items which we would normally expect to use in a sentence if we followed the grammatical rules.）。出處 口語裡的省略（[Are] You ready?）多益不太會拿來考填空；下面挑的是書面、職場文件裡常見、而且拿掉之後句子還要寫對的幾種。

副詞子句省略主詞（和 be）：When asked、Once approved、Although expensive

when、while、once、after、before、if、although 帶的子句，主詞跟主要子句相同時，可以把主詞（和 be 動詞）拿掉，只留 -ing、過去分詞或形容詞。劍橋：「很多表示時間的連接詞後面可以接 -ing 或 -ed 形式，代替主詞＋動詞」（Many of these time conjunctions can be followed by -ing or -ed forms instead of subject + verb.），這一頁列的時間連接詞包括 when、after、before、until、since、while、once、as、as soon as。出處 另一頁說「主詞跟主要子句的主詞相同時，常用這種沒有時態的子句」（We often use a non-finite clause when the subject is the same as the subject in the main clause），並說 after、although、though、if 這些連接詞後面常接這種子句，例子 The proposal, if accepted by Parliament, will mean fundamental changes to the education system.、By the end of the day, although exhausted, Mark did not feel quite as tired as he had in the past.（＝although he was exhausted）。出處

  • When asked about the delay, the spokesperson declined to comment.（被問到延誤時，發言人不予置評。）＝When she was asked…，被動留過去分詞。
  • Once approved, the budget will be sent to the finance department.（預算一經核准，就會送到財務部。）＝Once it is approved…。
  • If accepted, the offer will take effect on May 1.（如果接受，報價從 5 月 1 日生效。）
  • While waiting for the client, Ms. Kao reviewed the contract.（等客戶的時候，高小姐把合約看了一遍。）主動留 -ing。
  • Although more expensive, the new printer is much faster.（新印表機雖然比較貴，但快很多。）劍橋的例子 Though more expensive, the new model is safer and more efficient.。出處
  • While in Singapore, Mr. Park visited two of our suppliers.（在新加坡的時候，朴先生拜訪了兩家供應商。）同理，while he was in 拿掉 he was，留下介系詞片語。

前提是兩個子句的主詞要一樣：拿掉的主詞會被當成主要子句的主詞。這個「主詞相同」的要求針對的是 When asked、While waiting 這類留下分詞或形容詞的省略；下面的 if necessary、if possible、as requested、unless otherwise stated 是固定說法，不受這條限制。主詞不同還硬省略，就是〈分詞與分詞構句〉講過的懸垂分詞。主動用 -ing、被動用過去分詞的判斷，也跟那課一樣。

只拿掉主詞、留下有時態的動詞，不行：英國文化協會在講 and、but、or 後面省略主詞時提醒：這種省略「在其他連接詞後面不適用，例如 before、after、because、when、while」（Note that ellipsis doesn't work after other linkers, such as before, after, because, when and while.），例子是對的 We watch TV after we have dinner. 與錯的 We watch TV after have dinner.。出處 它說的是「只拿掉主詞、動詞照留」那種省略；上面 When asked、While waiting 是連 be 一起拿掉、改成沒有時態的分詞，是另一回事。要省就改成沒有時態的 -ing：after having dinner（劍橋的例子 I had something to eat before leaving.）。出處

if 後面的固定省略：If necessary、If possible、If so、If not

劍橋：「if 後面有時候可以省略一些字，形成固定說法」（We can sometimes leave words out after if to form fixed expressions），例子 Check the temperature of your meat with a meat thermometer if possible.（＝if it’s possible）；「意思很明顯時，if 後面用 so 或 not」（We use so or not after if when it is obvious what we are referring to），例子 If not, please email the office.。出處

  • If necessary, we can change the delivery date.（必要的話，可以改交貨日。）
  • Please reply by Friday if possible.（可以的話請在週五前回覆。）
  • Have you received the invoice? If so, please confirm the amount. If not, let me know.（收到發票了嗎？收到的話請確認金額；沒收到請告訴我。）

and、but、or 後面省略重複的主詞

英國文化協會：「and、but、or 後面不必重複主詞」（We don't have to repeat the subject after the linkers and, but and or.），主詞配 be 動詞時「主詞和 be 都可以省」（If the repeated subject is with the verb be, we can omit both the subject and be.）。出處 劍橋也說用 and、but、or 連接的子句不重複的字，「讀的人知道少掉的是什麼」（The same happens when we do not repeat words in clauses connected with and, but and or (coordinated clauses). We understand what the ‘missing’ items are）。出處

  • Ms. Lai reviewed the proposal and approved it the same day.（賴小姐看完提案、當天就核准了。）and 後面省略 she。
  • The new system is faster and easier to use.（新系統比較快、也比較好用。）省略 it is。
  • She can speak French, and her manager Spanish.（她會講法文，她的經理會講西班牙文。）同理，後半省略重複的 can speak，正式書面才這樣寫，讀到時知道少了什麼就好。

to 代替整個不定詞：I'd like to

前面講過的動作，後面不想重複，可以只留 to。英國文化協會：「to 後面常常可以不重複動詞片語的一部分」（We can often avoid repeating part of a verb phrase after to.），例子 Some people give up sugar completely, but I didn't want to [give up sugar completely].。出處

  • Would you like to join us for lunch? — I'd love to.（要一起吃午餐嗎？——好啊。）
  • I haven't finished the report yet, but I plan to this afternoon.（報告還沒寫完，但我打算今天下午寫完。）
  • You don't have to attend, but you're welcome to.（你不一定要參加，但歡迎你來。）

比較句的省略：than expected、than usual、as planned

劍橋：than 後面的子句「通常是省略過的子句」（The clause (underlined) is usually a reduced clause (a clause with ellipsis)），例子 The journey took longer than expected.（＝than people expected it to take）。出處 劍橋辭典的例句：If all goes ahead as planned, the new superstore will open in November.、Installing the new computer system has taken far longer than planned.出處；There was more rainfall than usual this summer in the mountain areas.出處。

  • Sales were higher than expected in the third quarter.（第三季的業績比預期高。）
  • The renovation was completed as planned.（整修照計畫完成了。）
  • The office will close earlier than usual on Friday.（辦公室週五會比平常早關。）

注意 than 和 then：比較用 than（than expected），then 是「然後、那時」。比較級的句型在〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉。

文件裡常見的省略片語：few, if any；unless otherwise stated；as requested

  • little／few, if any：「就算有也很少」。劍橋辭典：if「也用來講某樣東西的數量或程度」（If is also used to talk about the amount or degree of something），例句 This time of year we get little, if any, rain (= almost none).。出處 職場例句：Few, if any, of the applicants had experience with the software.（應徵者裡就算有人用過這套軟體，也沒幾個。）
  • unless otherwise stated：「除非另有說明」，省略的是 it is。牛津學習者辭典 otherwise 條目的例句 Unless otherwise stated, all translations in the text are my own.。出處 同理的寫法還有 unless otherwise noted、unless otherwise specified。
  • as requested、as needed、as agreed：跟 as planned 同一種形狀（as＋過去分詞），省略的是主詞和 be，例如 As requested, I have attached the price list.（依您的要求，附上價目表。）、Replace the filter as needed.（視需要更換濾網。）。

省略的前提：意思不會變、讀的人知道少了什麼。副詞子句省主詞（When asked、While waiting 這類）要「主詞相同」並改成 -ing、過去分詞或形容詞；不能只拿掉主詞而留下有時態的動詞。if necessary、as requested 這類固定說法不受「主詞相同」限制。

When ______ by reporters about the delay, the spokesperson declined to comment.

  • asking
  • asked
  • ask
  • was asked

發言人是「被」記者問（by reporters），原句 When she was asked…，省略主詞和 was 後留過去分詞 asked。asking 是主動；ask 是原形；was asked 前面少了主詞，不能只拿掉主詞留下有時態的動詞。

______ approved, the budget will be sent to the finance department.

  • During
  • Despite
  • Due to
  • Once

Once approved＝Once it is approved（一經核准），once 是連接詞，後面可以接省略過的過去分詞。During、Despite、Due to 是介系詞，後面要接名詞，不接 approved。

Please call the help desk ______ necessary.

  • if
  • so
  • than
  • for

if necessary（必要的話）是 if 後面省略 it is 的固定說法。so、than、for 後面都不接 necessary 這樣用。

While ______ Singapore, Mr. Park visited two of our suppliers.

  • was in
  • he in
  • in
  • to be in

While he was in Singapore 省略主詞和 was，留下 in Singapore。was in 留下有時態的動詞卻少了主詞；he in 少了 be 動詞；while 後面不接 to 不定詞。

We'll review the contract after ______ lunch.

  • have
  • had
  • having
  • has

after 後面不能只拿掉主詞、留下有時態的動詞（after have lunch 是錯的）；要省略就改成 -ing：after having lunch（after 在這裡也可以當介系詞，後面接 -ing）。

The installation took longer ______ expected.

  • as
  • then
  • that
  • than

longer 是比較級，配 than：than expected（比預期的久）。then 是「然後」；as 要配 as long as；that 不接在比較級後面。

Despite the bad weather, the product launch went ahead ______ planned.

  • as
  • than
  • so
  • such

as planned（照計畫）是 as 後面省略 it was 的說法。than 要配比較級；so、such 不這樣接 planned。

I haven't reviewed the report yet, but I plan ______ this afternoon.

  • that
  • to
  • for
  • so

plan to review the report 的 review the report 前面說過了，只留 to：I plan to。plan 後面不接 that 代替動作；for、so 也不這樣用。

Few, ______ any, of the applicants had experience with the software.

  • of
  • as
  • if
  • so

few, if any＝就算有也很少，是 if there were any 的省略。of、as、so 都沒有這個說法。

Unless otherwise ______, all prices include tax.

  • stated
  • stating
  • state
  • states

unless otherwise stated＝unless it is otherwise stated（除非另有說明），價格是「被說明」，留過去分詞 stated。stating 是主動；state、states 是有時態的動詞卻少了主詞。

整套文法怎麼用在考場

多益文法題的做題順序：先看選項，再看空格前後

先看考試本身。ETS 說閱讀測驗由考生「讀各種文字材料，自己控制速度作答（共 100 題）」（Test takers read a variety of materials and respond at their own pace (100 items total).），分成第五部分句子填空（Incomplete Sentences）、第六部分段落填空（Text Completion）、第七部分閱讀理解。出處 考生手冊寫明第五部分 30 題、第六部分 16 題；第五部分的題目說明是「每句少了一個字或片語，下面有四個選項」（A word or phrase is missing in each of the following sentences. Four answer choices are given below each sentence.），第六部分是「閱讀下面的文章，有些句子少了一個字或片語，每句下面有四個選項，選最適合的答案把文章補完整」（Read the texts that follow. A word or phrase is missing in some of the sentences. Four answer choices are given below each of the sentences. Select the best answer to complete the text.）。手冊第六部分的範例是一封電子郵件，裡面四個空格，其中一格的四個選項是完整的句子。出處

ETS 沒有公布哪種文法題出得最多，所以下面是解題建議，不是考試規則：照這個順序想，每一步都能對回這套教材的某一課。

四個步驟

  1. 先看四個選項，不急著讀整句。四個選項長什麼樣子，大致就決定這題考什麼（下表）。同字根不同詞性、同一個動詞不同時態、連接詞和介系詞混在一起，各有各的看法。
  2. 照那一型，到空格前後找線索。詞性題看空格前後一兩個字；時態題找時間詞（yesterday、since、by next year）；連接詞題看空格後面接子句還是名詞；關係詞題看先行詞是人還是東西、後面缺主詞還是缺受詞。
  3. 把選的答案放回句子讀一遍。檢查主詞和動詞單複數一致、主動還是被動、句子是不是只有一個有時態的主要動詞。
  4. 四個選項意思都不一樣、文法都對時，是字彙題。這時才需要讀懂整句意思，看搭配（apply for、comply with）和上下文。

第六部分的建議：做法一樣，但線索可能不在同一句——時態要對上整封信講的時間、代名詞要對上前文指的人、however／therefore 這類連接副詞要看前後兩句意思是相反還是因果；選整句的那一格，要讀前後文看哪一句接得上。

看到這種選項，想到哪一課

四個選項長這樣 → 回去複習先看哪裡
同字根不同詞性：success／successful／successfully／succeed
→ 〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉		空格前後：冠詞、所有格、介系詞後面填名詞；冠詞和名詞之間填形容詞；be 後面填形容詞；修飾動詞或形容詞填副詞
-ing 對 -ed 形容詞：satisfying／satisfied、attached／attaching
→ 〈分詞與分詞構句〉		名詞自己做還是被做；說東西還是說人的感受
名詞單複數、冠詞、數量詞：information／informations、much／many／each／a few
→ 〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉		名詞可不可數、單數還是複數
代名詞的格：he／him／his／himself
→ 〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉		空格在動詞前（主格）、動詞或介系詞後（受格）、名詞前（所有格）
同一個動詞不同時態：signed／has signed／will sign／signs
→ 〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉〈進行式三態〉〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉；單複數看〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉		時間線索（ago、since、by the time、next week），再看主詞單複數
主動被動混在一起：approved／was approved／approving／has approved
→ 〈被動語態〉		主詞是做事的還是被做的；空格後面有沒有受詞
情態助動詞：must／don't have to／should／might，或助動詞後面的動詞形式
→ 〈助動詞〉		意思是義務、不必、建議還是推測；助動詞後面接原形
to do／doing／do／done
→ 〈不定詞與動名詞〉		前面的動詞要接 to 還是 -ing；前面是不是介系詞（含 look forward to）
形容詞與副詞的比較：more／most／-er／as、much／very
→ 〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉		後面有沒有 than、前面有沒有 the、比較的是兩個還是一群
介系詞：at／on／in／by／until／for／during
→ 〈介系詞〉		後面是時刻、日期還是一段時間；是期限還是一直持續
連接詞與介系詞混在一起：although／despite／because／because of
→ 〈連接詞與連接副詞〉〈介系詞〉		空格後面是子句（有主詞＋有時態的動詞）還是名詞
連接副詞：however／therefore／moreover／otherwise
→ 〈連接詞與連接副詞〉		前後兩句意思是相反、因果還是補充；標點（句號、分號後面接）
關係詞：who／which／whose／where
→ 〈關係詞〉		先行詞是人、東西還是地點；後面直接接動詞＝缺主詞；後面是主詞＋動詞而缺受詞用 which、that；後面的名詞是先行詞「的」東西、前面沒有 the、a，用 whose；後面是完整的句子用 where、when
what／that／whether／if 開頭的子句，或疑問詞後面的語序
→ 〈名詞子句〉〈關係詞〉		前面有沒有先行詞；子句當主詞或受詞；間接問句用直述句語序
would／would have／had／were 和 if 子句的時態
→ 〈假設語氣〉		if 子句和主要子句的時態要成對；有沒有跟事實相反
句首是 Never／Not only／Only after／Should／Had，選項是同幾個字不同語序
→ 本課（倒裝）		句首的字會不會引起倒裝；倒裝時有沒有補 do／did
四個意思不同的單字：confirm／delay／cancel／reserve
→ 字彙題，不是文法題		整句意思與搭配

表裡「先看哪裡」那一欄是每課的判斷法縮成一句；例外與細節要回到那一課看，例如副詞也能放在句尾、連接詞後面也可能接 -ing。

四個選項是 although／despite／because／due to。先看空格的哪裡最快？

  • 空格前面是不是 the
  • 空格後面接的是子句還是名詞
  • 句子裡有沒有時間詞
  • 主詞是單數還是複數

連接詞（although、because）後面接子句，介系詞（despite、due to）後面接名詞，先看空格後面就能刪掉一半；剩下兩個再看意思是讓步還是原因。（〈連接詞與連接副詞〉）

四個選項是 reviews／reviewed／is reviewed／has reviewed，空格後面直接接句號。最該先想的是什麼？

  • 這格填名詞還是形容詞
  • 介系詞該用哪一個
  • 主詞是做事的還是被做的
  • 先行詞是人還是東西

選項裡有主動也有被動，空格後面又沒有受詞，先判斷主詞是被做的那一方（例如 The report…）就該用被動；再用時間線索決定時態。（〈被動語態〉）

綜合練習

綜合練習：一題考一課，照多益第五部分的樣子

下面每一題考這套文法教材其中一課的一個觀念，題目順序照課程順序排。先照上一段的順序做：看選項是哪一型、到空格前後找線索。解析最後的括號是那一課的課名，錯了就回去那一課複習。

The new software allows staff to complete their reports more ______.

  • efficient
  • efficiently
  • efficiency
  • efficiencies

more ______ 修飾動詞 complete（怎麼完成），用副詞 efficiently。efficient 是形容詞；efficiency、efficiencies 是名詞。（〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉）

We need more ______ before we can make a final decision.

  • information
  • informations
  • an information
  • the informations

information 是不可數名詞，不加 -s、不加 a／an。（〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉）

______ applicant must submit two letters of reference.

  • All
  • Many
  • Both
  • Each

applicant 是單數可數名詞，前面用 each。all、many、both 後面要接複數名詞（all applicants）。（〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉）

The manager asked Mr. Kim and ______ to prepare the slides.

  • I
  • me
  • my
  • mine

asked 的受詞是 Mr. Kim and ___，用受格 me；遮住 Mr. Kim and 再讀一次：asked me。I 是主格；my 後面要接名詞；mine 是所有格代名詞。（〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉）

The list of approved suppliers ______ updated at the start of every month.

  • is
  • are
  • were
  • be

真正的主詞是 the list（單數），of approved suppliers 只是修飾；every month 是習慣，用現在式 is。are、were 是複數；be 不是有時態的動詞。（〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉）

Mr. Ruiz ______ the contract two days ago.

  • has signed
  • signed
  • signs
  • will sign

two days ago 是過去明確的時間，用過去簡單式 signed；ago 不配現在完成式。（〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉）

When the fire alarm went off, the staff ______ a product demonstration.

  • attend
  • are attending
  • have attended
  • were attending

警報響的那一刻，訓練正在進行：長動作用過去進行式 were attending，被 when 帶出的短動作（went off）打斷。attend、are attending、have attended 都不是過去的時間。（〈進行式三態〉）

Our company ______ in this building since 2015.

  • has been
  • is
  • was
  • will be

since 2015＝從 2015 年一路到現在，用現在完成式 has been。（〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉）

By the time the client arrived, the team ______ the meeting room.

  • prepares
  • will prepare
  • had prepared
  • has prepared

by the time＋過去式（arrived）：客戶到之前就準備好了，先發生的用過去完成式 had prepared。has prepared 是一路算到現在，對不上過去的參考點。（〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉）

The technician will start the installation once the new server ______ tomorrow morning.

  • will arrive
  • arrived
  • arrives
  • would arrive

once 帶的時間子句講未來（tomorrow morning），用現在式 arrives，不用 will。arrived、would arrive 對不上主要子句的 will start。（〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉）

By next March, Ms. Ho ______ at the firm for ten years.

  • has worked
  • works
  • worked
  • will have worked

by next March 是未來的參考點，一路算到那時滿十年，用未來完成式 will have worked。（〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉）

The quarterly report ______ to all shareholders last week.

  • was sent
  • sent
  • is sending
  • has sent

報告是「被寄出」的，空格後面也沒有受詞，用被動；last week 是過去，was sent。sent、has sent 是主動，會變成報告去寄東西；is sending 是主動進行式。（〈被動語態〉）

The new safety rules ______ by all factory workers starting next month.

  • will follow
  • are following
  • will be followed
  • have followed

規定是「被遵守」的，後面是 by all factory workers，用被動；starting next month 是未來：will be followed。其他三個都是主動，會變成規定去遵守別人。（〈被動語態〉）

You ______ print your ticket, but you can if you'd like a paper copy. The e-ticket on your phone is enough.

  • mustn't
  • don't have to
  • can't
  • have to

手機上的電子票就夠了、想印也可以（you can if you'd like a paper copy），所以是「不必」印：don't have to。mustn't、can't 是「禁止」，跟「想印也可以」矛盾；have to 是「一定要」，跟「電子票就夠了」矛盾。（〈助動詞〉）

The project ______ have been finished by now, but it's still not done.

  • should
  • must
  • can't
  • will

should have＋過去分詞＝本來應該做完、卻沒做完，配後半句 it's still not done。must have been finished 是「一定已經做完了」，跟後半句矛盾；can't have 是「不可能已經做完」，跟 but 的轉折對不上；will have been finished by now 是推測「現在應該已經做完了」，跟 it's still not done 矛盾。（〈助動詞〉）

The committee decided ______ the event until May.

  • postponing
  • postpone
  • to postpone
  • postponed

decide 後面接 to＋原形：decided to postpone。（〈不定詞與動名詞〉）

We look forward to ______ you at the conference.

  • see
  • seeing
  • seen
  • be seen

look forward to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接 -ing：seeing。（〈不定詞與動名詞〉）

Please read the ______ instructions before installing the software.

  • attached
  • attaching
  • attach
  • attaches

說明是「被附上」的，名詞前用過去分詞 attached。attaching 是主動，會變成說明去附加東西；attach、attaches 是動詞。（〈分詞與分詞構句〉）

This year's sales are ______ higher than last year's.

  • very
  • more
  • most
  • much

加強比較級用 much（far、a lot、even），不用 very；higher 已經是比較級，前面不再加 more；most 是最高級。（〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉）

Of all the candidates, Ms. Lee has the ______ experience in international sales.

  • most
  • more
  • much
  • many

of all the candidates 是在一群人裡比，用最高級 the most。more 是兩者比；the much、the many 沒有這種用法。（〈形容詞與副詞：比較級與最高級〉）

The store opens ______ 9 a.m. on weekdays.

  • in
  • at
  • on
  • for

時刻用 at：at 9 a.m.。on 用在星期幾和日期；in 用在時段、月、年；for 接一段時間。（〈介系詞〉）

Please submit your timesheet ______ Friday at the latest.

  • until
  • since
  • during
  • by

at the latest（最晚）說的是期限，用 by：最晚週五前交。until 是一直持續到那時；since 配完成式講起點；during 接一段期間或事件。（〈介系詞〉）

The printer is ten years old; ______, it still works well.

  • although
  • however
  • despite
  • because

分號後面、逗號前面放連接副詞 however（然而）：舊卻還好用，前後相反。although、because 是連接詞，後面不加逗號；despite 是介系詞，後面要接名詞。（〈連接詞與連接副詞〉）

The engineer ______ designed the cooling system will lead the training.

  • which
  • whose
  • who
  • whom

先行詞 the engineer 是人，空格後面直接接動詞 designed（缺主詞），用 who。which 指東西；whose 後面要接名詞；whom 是受格，不能當主詞。（〈關係詞〉）

This is the hotel ______ we held last year's sales conference.

  • which
  • what
  • who
  • where

空格後面 we held last year's sales conference 是完整的句子（不缺受詞），指地點用 where（＝in which）。which 要後面缺受詞或補介系詞；what 前面不能有先行詞；who 指人。（〈關係詞〉）

Could you tell me ______?

  • where is the meeting room
  • where the meeting room is
  • where does the meeting room
  • that the meeting room is

問句放進句子裡變成間接問句，用直述句語序：疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞，where the meeting room is。where is the meeting room 是直接問句的語序；where does the meeting room 用了問句的 does，而且少了動詞；that the meeting room is 沒有說是「在哪裡」，句子不完整。（〈名詞子句〉）

______ the new policy will reduce costs is still unclear.

  • Whether
  • If
  • What
  • Which

「新政策會不會降低成本」是一個是非問題，放在句首當主詞，只能用 Whether，不能用 If。What、Which 後面的子句要缺東西，這裡 the new policy will reduce costs 是完整的句子。（〈名詞子句〉）

If we ______ the order yesterday instead of today, it would have arrived by now.

  • placed
  • have placed
  • would place
  • had placed

主要子句是 would have arrived，講跟過去事實相反（昨天其實沒下單，今天才下），if 子句用 had＋過去分詞：had placed。placed 會變成第二類的形式，對不上 would have；have placed、would place 不放在這種 if 子句。（〈假設語氣〉）

If the shipment ______ late, our team will contact you right away.

  • arrives
  • will arrive
  • arrived
  • would arrive

主要子句是 will contact，講未來真的可能發生的事，if 子句用現在式 arrives，不用 will。arrived、would arrive 是跟事實相反的形式，對不上 will。（〈假設語氣〉）

重點整理

  • 倒裝＝句首的字＋助動詞（或 be）＋主詞，語序跟問句一樣；沒有助動詞就補 do／does／did，動詞回原形。用在正式的書面與致詞。
  • 否定副詞放句首要倒裝：Never、Rarely、Seldom、Little、Not only（倒裝只在這一半）、Under no circumstances、At no time、Not until。Hardly／Scarcely…when、No sooner…than 通常配過去完成式。Only after／when／then 倒裝的是後面的主要子句；Only 只修飾主詞（Only the manager can…）不倒裝。
  • 省略 if 的條件句倒裝：Should＋主詞＋原形（第一類）、Were＋主詞（＋to 原形）（第二類）、Had＋主詞＋過去分詞（第三類）；否定 not 放主詞後面（Had I not）。完整講法在〈假設語氣〉。
  • 附和句：So／Neither／Nor＋助動詞＋主詞，助動詞跟前一句對上（met → did、hasn't → has）；不倒裝的說法是 not … either。Here comes＋名詞，但代名詞不倒裝（Here it is）；Attached is／are 的 be 動詞跟後面的主詞一致。
  • 省略：副詞子句主詞相同時可以省主詞和 be（When asked、Once approved、Although expensive、While in…）；if necessary、as requested 這類固定說法不受主詞相同的限制；不能只拿掉主詞而留下有時態的動詞（after having lunch，不是 after have lunch）。if necessary／if possible／if so／if not；than expected、than usual、as planned；I'd like to；few, if any；unless otherwise stated。
  • 做題順序（建議）：先看四個選項是哪一型 → 到空格前後找那一型的線索 → 放回句子檢查單複數、主被動、只有一個主要動詞 → 選項意思都不同才是字彙題。第六部分的線索可能在前後句。
