開場：Tell me about yourself

兩分鐘自我介紹：Present–Past–Future

自我介紹照 present-past-future 三段講，不要只是重述履歷：出處

  • Present：先簡短講現在在做什麼
  • Past：講怎麼走到這裡，突顯你練出來的技能，說明它們怎麼用在這份工作
  • Future：帶到未來的目標

這跟〈行為面試與專題表達〉的三段（現在做什麼、兩件代表作、為什麼是這裡）是同一個形狀。

段落常用句型範例（虛構）
Present 現在I'm a … with … years of experience.
I work on …		I'm a backend engineer with three years of experience. I work on a batch reconciliation system built with Spring Boot.
Past 代表作Two things I'm proud of. First, I … Second, I …First, I cut our month-end batch from six hours to two and a half by adding indexes and running independent steps in parallel. Second, I helped move six services to Kubernetes. I migrated the first one and turned what I learned into a template for the team.
Future 為什麼是這裡Next, I want to … That's why I applied for this role.Next, I want to work on systems with higher transaction volume and stricter correctness requirements. That's why I applied for this role.

三個時態各管一段（選法見〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉與〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉）：

  • Present：現在式（I work on）或現在完成式（I've worked on … for three years）
  • Past：講已經做完的事，用過去式（cut、helped、migrated）
  • Future：want to、I'm looking for 這類說法

範例大約 90 個字，念起來不到一分鐘。留下的時間給每件代表作多講一句做法或數字，不要多塞第三件。

英文自我介紹照 present-past-future，第一句最適合講什麼？

  • 在哪裡出生、念哪所小學
  • 現在的職位與負責的系統
  • 為什麼想來這家公司
  • 從大學到現在每一份工作，照時間順序講

先簡短講現在在做什麼。為什麼想來放最後；照時間把每份工作講一遍就是在重述履歷。

Past 那一段講代表作，動詞用哪個時態最自然？

First, I ___ our month-end batch from six hours to two and a half.
  • cut，過去式
  • am cutting，現在進行式
  • will cut，未來式
  • cutting，只寫 -ing

已經做完的一件事，用過去式。cut 的過去式跟原形同形；cutting 沒有時態，句子會少了主要動詞。

哪一句最適合放在 Future 那一段收尾？

  • Next, I want to work on larger systems. That's why I applied.
  • I was born in Taichung, and I grew up there with my parents and two brothers.
  • I have three years of experience, mostly with Java, Spring Boot and Oracle.
  • I graduated from university in 2020.

Future 要把下一步跟這個職位接起來。年資放 Present，畢業屬於過去經歷；出生地跟職位無關。

回答 Tell me about yourself 要避免什麼？

  • 提到現在的工作
  • 提到未來的目標
  • 講超過三十秒
  • 只是重述履歷

不要只是重述履歷，也不要講太私人的事；要講經驗與技能怎麼對上這份工作。

哪一句最像 Present 段的開頭？

  • I'm a backend engineer with three years of experience.
  • I'm backend engineer with three years experience.
  • I'm a backend engineer with three years experiences.
  • I'm a backend engineer since three years, working on payments.

說職業要加 a／an（〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉）；年資常說 three years of experience，experience 指「累積的知識與技能」時不可數，不加 s；since 後面接起點，不接一段時間。

範例大約 90 個字，念起來不到一分鐘。時間還有剩，想多講一點，比較好的做法是？

  • 補上從大學開始的每一份工作
  • 多講興趣與家庭
  • 每件代表作多講一句做法或數字
  • 加第三、第四件代表作，各講一句

兩件代表作是你希望被追問的地方，把它們講具體比多塞幾件好；照時間補每份工作就變成念履歷。

講專案：STAR

用 STAR 把一個 Java 後端專案講成英文故事

STAR 是 Situation、Task、Action、Result 的縮寫。

  • 由來：美國的人才評鑑顧問公司 DDI 自稱在 1974 年隨 Targeted Selection 推出 STAR；DDI 拆成三段：ST（情境或任務）、A（行動）、R（結果）。出處
  • 現在職涯中心教的多半拆成四段。
  • 時間分配建議：情境 20%、任務 10%、行動 60%、結果 10%，回答的大部分放在你的行動。出處〈行為面試與專題表達〉也照這個比例。

另外兩個重點直接影響英文怎麼講：出處

  • 講自己的貢獻用 I：用 we 會讓雇主很難看清楚你的技能
  • 結果要突顯可以量化的成果

下面用〈行為面試與專題表達〉那個轉帳重複處理的虛構故事：

段落英文（虛構範例）中文意思
SituationLast year, our transfer API processed the same transfer twice. A client timed out and resent the request, and our service handled both.去年轉帳 API 把同一筆處理了兩次：呼叫端逾時重送，服務兩次都處理了。
TaskI owned that API, so it was my job to fix it and make sure it never happened again.我負責那支 API，要修好並確保不再發生。
ActionFirst, I reversed the duplicate transfer to stop the damage. Then I added an idempotency key. I had the client send a unique request ID, and I stored it with a unique constraint, so a repeated ID returned the original result instead of creating a new transfer. I also wrote tests that sent the same request twice.先把重複的那筆沖正止血；再加冪等鍵：我讓呼叫端送唯一的請求編號，存進有唯一限制的欄位，重複的編號就回傳原本的結果，不會再建一筆。也寫了同一個請求送兩次的測試。
ResultIt hasn't happened since, and "every external API needs an idempotency key" is now on our team's review checklist.之後沒再發生，「對外 API 一律要冪等鍵」進了團隊的檢查清單。

幾個寫法上的重點：

  • 故事已經發生，Situation 到 Action 用過去式
  • Result 講「到現在為止」的狀態，用現在完成式 hasn't happened since 很自然，since 一路算到現在（〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉）
  • Action 用 First, … Then, … I also … 把步驟串起來，每一步主詞都是 I
  • 講專案常用的動詞：designed、implemented、migrated、reduced、cut、added、wrote、led
  • 有數字時用 from … to …：reduced the batch time from six hours to two and a half

講團隊專案時，哪一句最能講清楚你的貢獻？

  • I added an idempotency key.
  • We fixed the bug and we added some tests.
  • The bug was fixed and some tests were added.
  • Our team did a lot of work on it.

用 we 會讓雇主看不清楚你的技能。被動語態把做事的人藏起來，效果跟 we 一樣；I added an idempotency key 講清楚了誰、做了什麼。

STAR 哪一段應該講最久？

  • Situation
  • Task
  • Action
  • Result

建議的分配是 20%、10%、60%、10%，回答的大部分放在行動。

哪一句 Result 比較好？

  • The result was very good.
  • Everyone on the team was really happy with how it turned out.
  • It became much better than before.
  • The batch now takes 2.5 hours, not six.

結果要突顯可以量化的成果。其他三句都沒有可以檢驗的內容。

Situation 這句的空格填什麼？

Last year, our transfer API ___ the same transfer twice.
  • has processed
  • processes
  • is processing
  • processed

Last year 是明確的過去時間，用過去式；現在完成式不跟這類時間詞連用（〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉）。

Result 想說「從那之後就沒再發生」，哪一句對？

  • It didn't happen since.
  • It doesn't happen since.
  • It hasn't happened since two years.
  • It hasn't happened since.

since 一路算到現在，配現在完成式。since 後面要接起點（since last year），兩年這種一段時間要用 for。

DDI 自稱 STAR 是哪一年隨 Targeted Selection 推出的？

  • 1954 年
  • 1974 年
  • 1994 年
  • 2004 年

1974 年隨 Targeted Selection 推出。這是 DDI 自己的說法。

Action 想說「我把月結批次從六小時縮到兩個半小時」，哪一句最自然？

  • I reduced the batch from six hours become two and a half hours.
  • I reduced the batch time six hours to two and a half.
  • I reduced the batch time from six hours to two and a half.
  • I made the batch reduce from six hours to two and a half.

數字變化用 from … to …，兩個介系詞都不能少。become 多了一個動詞；reduce 直接接受詞（reduced the batch time），不用 make … reduce 繞一圈。

技術名詞：再講清楚

用一兩句英文解釋技術名詞

面試官常會說 Can you explain what … means?。好的回答是一句定義加一個例子。下表左欄的英文是本課自編的口語說法，右欄是正式的意思。

名詞一句話說法（自編）正式的意思
idempotent（冪等）An idempotent request has the same effect whether you send it once or many times. For example, retrying a payment with the same request ID shouldn't charge the customer twice.多個相同請求對伺服器的預期效果，跟只送一個相同出處
race condition（競爭條件）A race condition happens when two threads work on the same data at the same time and the result depends on which one runs first. For example, two threads read a balance of 100 and both write back their own update, so one update is lost.Java 裡這類問題也叫 thread interference：不同執行緒對同一份資料的兩個操作交錯執行時發生出處
eventual consistency（最終一致性）With eventual consistency, a read right after a write might return old data, but all copies catch up after a short time.最終一致的讀取，結果可能沒反映剛完成的寫入出處
latency / throughputLatency is how long one request takes. Throughput is how much work the system finishes in a period of time.latency：送出指令後、資料開始傳之前的延遲出處；throughput：一段時間內完成的工作量出處
legacy systemIt's a legacy system: it's old, but replacing it would be too risky or expensive, so we still maintain it.已經買不到、但因為換掉太難或太貴而仍在使用的電腦系統出處
deprecatedThat method is deprecated, so we moved to the new API before it gets removed.deprecate 的電腦用法：被認為過時、最好避免，即使還能用出處；別跟 depreciate（貶值）搞混出處
scalable / bottleneckThe database was the bottleneck, so we added a cache to make the service more scalable.scalable：能調整以應付未來更大需求的電腦、網路或軟體出處；bottleneck：原意是道路變窄、車流變慢的地方，另一個意思是拖慢發展或進度的任何東西出處

冪等與最終一致性在面試集〈系統設計：訊息佇列與最終一致性〉有完整的中文說明，這裡只練英文怎麼講。

面試官問 What does idempotent mean?，哪個回答最好？

  • It means the API always responds quickly, even under heavy load.
  • Repeating the same request has the same effect as sending it once.
  • It means the request never fails, so the client doesn't need to retry.
  • It means only one user can call it at a time.

多個相同請求的效果跟一個相同。冪等不保證快、不保證不失敗，也不是限制同時只有一個人呼叫。

哪一句在描述 race condition？

  • The service is slow because the database is far away.
  • The data is old because the cache hasn't been refreshed for a few minutes.
  • The API returned an error because the input was invalid.
  • Two threads updated the same balance and one update was lost.

兩個執行緒對同一份資料交錯操作，結果看誰先誰後，就是競爭條件。快取沒更新是另一回事。

用 eventual consistency 的系統，剛寫完馬上讀，可能怎樣？

  • 一定讀到剛寫的值
  • 讀取會失敗，要等所有副本都寫完才能再讀
  • 寫入會被還原
  • 可能讀到舊的值，過一下就會一致

最終一致的讀取，結果可能沒反映剛完成的寫入。不會報錯，只是可能是舊的。

「這個方法已經過時、不建議再用」，英文用哪個字？

  • depreciated
  • deprecated
  • decreased
  • degraded

deprecate 有被認為過時的意思；depreciate 是「貶值」，常被混用但意思不同。

Latency 和 throughput 的差別，哪一句講得對？

  • Latency is how long one request takes; throughput is how much work gets done in a period of time.
  • Latency and throughput are the same thing.
  • Latency is how many requests the system handles per second; throughput is how long one request takes.
  • Throughput is the delay before data starts to move.

latency 是延遲，throughput 是一段時間的工作量。說 latency 是每秒處理幾個請求的那句把兩個定義對調了；說 throughput 是資料開始傳之前的延遲，是把 latency 的定義安錯了地方。

面試時說 We still maintain a legacy system.，legacy 在這裡的意思是？

  • 前任主管留下的遺產
  • 最新、最受重視的系統
  • 授權已經過期、依法不能再繼續使用的系統
  • 老舊、但換掉太難或太貴而繼續用的系統

legacy 當形容詞：老舊、但因為換掉太難或太貴而仍在使用。

常見問題

Why this company、weakness、conflict 與反問

面試常見的問題：出處

  • 開場：Tell me about yourself.、Why do you want to work here?、Why should we hire you?
  • 行為題：Tell me about a time when you handled a difficult situation with a co-worker.、Tell me about a time you made a mistake or failed at something.

下面四題各給回答方向與範本，範本是自編的，方括號是要換成你自己的內容。

Why this company?

這題最看你事前的研究：提到讓你有共鳴的具體面向，例如組織的價值觀、最近的專案或成長機會，再說明它們跟你的技能、興趣與目標怎麼接上。出處

I read that your team is [moving payments to an event-driven design]. I've spent the last year [building idempotent APIs and working with message queues], so I'd like to help with that, and I'd learn a lot from a team working at your scale.

What is your greatest weakness?

誠實回答，講行為不講個性，並講你正在怎麼改進。出處

挑哪一項來講：出處

  • weakness：挑一個這份工作不是非用不可、但也不是毫不相干的技能或特質
  • strengths 相反：挑跟職位相關的技能，準備用過的例子
  • strengths 避免 hard working、dedicated 這類籠統的特質

I used to read from my slides when I presented to large groups, so it was hard to keep people interested. Now I volunteer to give our team's monthly tech talk, and I practice with a colleague first.

Tell me about a conflict with a co-worker.

這是行為題，用 STAR。英文的重點是講「意見不同」而不是「吵架」，常用 We disagreed on …、Instead of arguing, I …、We agreed to …。範例沿用〈行為面試與專題表達〉的對帳欄位故事：

We disagreed on a new field in the reconciliation file. The upstream team wanted a string, and I thought it should be a number. Instead of arguing, I listed how each option would affect the three downstream systems. We agreed to use a number with a conversion step for compatibility, and we finished the spec on time.

Do you have any questions for us?

反問可以問：出處

  • What do you see as the challenges for the person taking this job?
  • Describe your ideal candidate to fill this position.
  • What do you enjoy most about working here?

工程職位可以再問（自編）：

  • How does the team review and release code?
  • What does a typical first month look like for this role?

反問清單的內容見〈行為面試與專題表達〉。

回答 Why do you want to work here?，哪一句比較好？

  • I've built idempotent APIs, and your team is rebuilding payments.
  • Because your company is famous and everyone in Taiwan knows its name.
  • Because the salary is good and the office is close to my home.
  • Because I need a job.

提到讓你有共鳴的具體面向（例如最近的專案），再接上你的技能。名氣、薪水、需要工作都不是這家公司特有的理由。

回答 weakness，哪一句最好？

  • I'm a perfectionist, so I sometimes work too hard and too late.
  • I'm not a confident person.
  • I don't have any weaknesses.
  • I used to read from my slides; now I practice first.

要講行為、不講個性，並講正在怎麼改進。「不夠有自信」是個性；說沒有缺點等於沒回答。

回答衝突題，哪一句開頭最好？

  • My co-worker was always wrong, so I had to fight with him.
  • We disagreed on a new field in the reconciliation file.
  • I never have conflicts with anyone.
  • Conflict is bad, so I always avoid it.

用 disagreed on 描述意見不同的具體事情，接著講你怎麼處理。說對方總是錯、或說從沒有衝突，都沒辦法展示處理能力。

回答 strengths，要避免哪種說法？

  • 跟應徵職位直接相關、職缺上寫到的技能
  • hard working 這類籠統特質
  • 舉出過去實際用過這個技能的具體例子
  • 可以帶到不同工作、不限一份職務的技能

要避免 hard working、dedicated 這類籠統的特質。其他三項都是建議要做的。

面試尾聲對方問 Do you have any questions for us?，哪個回應最好？

  • Not really. I think you've already covered everything I wanted to know.
  • How many days off do I get?
  • What do you see as the challenges for the person taking this job?
  • What does your company do?

這是常見的反問例子。公司做什麼應該事前查好；只問假期容易讓人覺得只在乎福利；直接說沒有問題浪費了這個機會。

下面哪一句英文最自然？

  • We had a disagree about the field type.
  • We were disagree on the field type.
  • We disagreed the field type.
  • We disagreed on the field type.

disagree 是動詞，名詞是 disagreement；were disagree 是 be 動詞加原形動詞，錯；disagree 不直接接受詞，要加 on 或 about。

weakness 範本 I used to read from my slides when I presented to large groups.，used to 在這裡表示？

  • 我已經習慣這樣做，現在仍然如此
  • 我被拿來用
  • 我以前會這樣，現在不再這樣
  • 我將會這樣做

used to＋原形動詞講過去的習慣，暗示現在已經不同，所以後面接 Now I …。「習慣於」是 be used to＋名詞或動名詞，形式不同，見〈不定詞與動名詞〉。

講正在改進的做法，哪一句時態最合適？

  • Now I practiced with a colleague first.
  • Now I practice with a colleague first.
  • Now I had practiced with a colleague first.
  • Now I will practiced with a colleague first.

現在的習慣用現在簡單式（〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉）。will 後面要原形；now 配過去式或過去完成式互相矛盾。

卡住的時候

聽不懂、沒聽清楚、需要想一下

聽不懂就問，比猜錯題目答一大段好。請對方重說或講清楚，可以用這幾個說法：

情況說法意思與用法
沒聽清楚Sorry, could you repeat the question?sorry 的感嘆詞用法之一：請對方重說沒聽清楚的話；英式英文較常用出處
沒聽清楚（短）Pardon?因為沒聽到或沒聽懂，請對方重說出處
聽到了但不懂Could you rephrase that?rephrase：換句話說，讓意思更清楚出處
確認理解Just to clarify, do you mean the read path or the write path?clarify：讓事情更清楚易懂出處
需要想一下That's a good question. Let me think for a moment.（自編）
沒碰過I haven't used it in production, but here's how I would approach it.（自編）

要避開的是非正式（informal）的 Come again?出處，以及只說一個 What?。請別人做事用 Could you …? 比 Can you …? 客氣，這在〈助動詞〉講過。

面試官語速很快，你沒聽清楚問題。哪一句最合適？

  • What? Your English is too fast.
  • Come again? I didn't catch that.
  • Sorry, could you repeat that?
  • Repeat.

sorry 可以用來請對方重說沒聽清楚的話，加上 could you 更客氣。Come again? 是非正式說法；What? 太直接，後半句還把問題推給對方。

每個字都聽到了，但不確定題目在問什麼。最合適的是？

  • Could you speak louder?
  • Could you rephrase that?
  • I can't hear you.
  • Pardon me for being late.

rephrase 是換句話說讓意思更清楚，正好對上「聽到了但不懂」。大聲一點、聽不到是解決沒聽清楚的問題。

面試官問 How would you make this faster?，你不確定指的是讀還是寫。哪一句最好？

  • I don't understand your question. Can you ask something else?
  • 直接挑一個猜，講完再問對方是不是要問這個
  • Just to clarify, do you mean the read path or the write path?
  • That's not a good question.

用 Just to clarify 把兩種可能說出來，對方只要選一個；只說不懂，對方不知道你卡在哪。

被問到沒在正式環境用過的技術，哪個回答最好？

  • Yes, I'm an expert in it. I've used it in every project I've worked on.
  • I haven't used it in production, but here's my approach.
  • No.
  • I don't know anything about it, sorry.

誠實說明經驗範圍，再展示你的思路。在面試裡扭曲事實有風險，可能跟履歷對不起來。

下面哪一句請求最客氣？

  • Could you say that again, please?
  • Say it again. I didn't hear the question.
  • Can you say it again?
  • You say again?

can you 也能用來請求，但比較隨便（〈助動詞〉）；could you 加 please 最客氣。祈使句與省略助動詞的問法都太直接。

常見錯誤

時態、冠詞與中式英文

下面是台灣工程師面試時常說錯的地方，每一條背後的文法都在英文模組前面講過：

不自然自然為什麼
I have joined the company in 2021.I joined the company in 2021.明確的過去時間（in 2021、last year、two years ago）配過去式，見〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉
I work here since 2021.I've worked here since 2021.從過去一路到現在，用現在完成式
I am backend engineer.I'm a backend engineer.說職業要加 a／an，見〈名詞、冠詞與數量詞〉
We discussed about the design.We discussed the design.discuss 直接接受詞；discuss about 仍被多數人、老師與考試視為錯誤出處；也見〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉
I got many feedbacks.I got a lot of feedback.feedback 不可數出處；information 也是出處
I'm responsible to the payment API.I'm responsible for the payment API.句型是 responsible for somebody/something、responsible for doing something出處
I have 3 years experiences.I have three years of experience.experience 指做一件事一段時間累積的知識與技能時不可數出處
I graduated NTU at 2020.I graduated from NTU in 2020.graduate 後面接 from（She graduated from Harvard this year.）；年份前用 in出處

美式另有 graduated college（不加 from）的說法，但面試用 graduated from 最保險。

這句哪裡不自然？

I have joined the company in 2021.
  • joined 要改成 join
  • in 要改成 at，年份前面用 at
  • 沒有問題，現在完成式可以配年份
  • in 2021 要配過去式 joined

現在完成式不跟明確的過去時間連用：I joined the company in 2021。

「我從 2021 年開始在這裡工作（到現在）」，哪一句對？

  • I work here since 2021.
  • I've worked here since 2021.
  • I worked here since 2021.
  • I'm working here from 2021 until now.

since 一路算到現在，配現在完成式。現在式與過去式都沒有「一路到現在」的意思。

這句哪裡不自然？

We discussed about the retry policy yesterday.
  • discuss 直接接受詞，about 要拿掉
  • yesterday 要改成 last day
  • discussed 要改成 have discussed
  • retry policy 要加 s

discuss about 仍被多數人視為錯誤，改說 discussed the retry policy 或 had a discussion about。yesterday 配過去式沒問題。

哪一句對？

  • The reviewers gave me many feedbacks.
  • The reviewers gave me a feedback.
  • The reviewers gave me a lot of useful feedback.
  • The reviewers gave me several useful feedbacks on my code.

feedback 不可數：不加 s、不加 a，也不用 many、several；用 a lot of、some。

「我負責支付 API」，哪一句對？

  • I'm responsible for the payment API.
  • I'm responsible to the payment API.
  • I'm responsible of the payment API.
  • I'm responsible about the payment API.

句型是 responsible for somebody/something。

這句哪裡不自然？

I am Java developer at a bank.
  • at 要改成 in，在銀行工作用 in
  • developer 前面少了 a
  • Java 要小寫
  • am 要改成 was

說職業用 a／an：I'm a Java developer at a bank。work at a bank 是常見說法，at 沒問題。

「我 2020 年從台大畢業」，哪一句最自然？

  • I graduated NTU at 2020.
  • I have graduated from NTU in 2020.
  • I graduated from NTU in 2020.
  • I was graduate from NTU in 2020.

graduate 接 from（She graduated from Harvard this year）；年份前用 in；in 2020 是明確的過去時間，不用現在完成式；was graduate 把名詞當動詞用了。

重點整理

  • 自我介紹照 present-past-future：現在的職位與系統（現在式或現在完成式）→ 兩件代表作（過去式、有數字）→ 下一步與為什麼是這個職位。不要重述履歷。
  • STAR 是 DDI 自稱在 1974 年提出的；時間分配建議情境 20%、任務 10%、行動 60%、結果 10%。行動用 I 不用 we，結果要能量化。故事用過去式，Result 講到現在用現在完成式（hasn't happened since）。
  • 念法：cache /kæʃ/、queue /kjuː/、schema /ˈskiːmə/、suite /swiːt/、integer 的 g 念 /dʒ/、asynchronous 重音在 syn、router 美式兩種都對。解釋名詞用「一句定義＋一個例子」；deprecated 不是 depreciated。
  • Why this company 講具體的專案或價值觀再接上自己；weakness 講行為不講個性，並說怎麼改；conflict 用 We disagreed on …；反問可用 What do you see as the challenges for the person taking this job?
  • 沒聽清楚：Sorry, could you repeat the question?；聽到但不懂：Could you rephrase that?；確認：Just to clarify, do you mean …?；避開 Come again?（非正式）與單獨一個 What?。
  • 常見錯誤：明確的過去時間配過去式；since 配現在完成式；職業加 a／an；discuss 不加 about；feedback、information、經驗的 experience 不可數；responsible for；graduated from … in 年份。
