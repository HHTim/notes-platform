一整個子句，當一個名詞用

名詞子句是什麼：四個位置、三種開頭

子句（clause）是有自己的主詞和動詞的一組字。劍橋：「主要子句通常由主詞（一個名詞片語）和動詞片語組成」（Typically a main clause is made up of a subject (s) (a noun phrase) and a verb phrase (v).）。出處

有些子句不單獨成句，而是整個放進另一個句子裡，站在名詞會站的位置：當主詞、當受詞、當補語，或放在介系詞後面。這種子句叫名詞子句（noun clause）。美國之音學英語節目的例子：Whether he wins is not my concern.，「這句的名詞子句是 Whether he wins，它是句子的主詞」（The noun clause in this sentence is "Whether he wins" and it is the subject of the sentence.）。出處

位置例句名詞子句
主詞What the client wants is a lower price.（客戶要的是更低的價格。）What the client wants
受詞We believe (that) the new system will save time.（我們相信新系統會省時間。）(that) the new system will save time
補語The problem is that the budget is too small.（問題是預算太少。）that the budget is too small
介系詞後面We talked about whether we should hire a contractor.（我們討論了要不要找外包。）whether we should hire a contractor

表格的根據：當受詞，劍橋說某些動詞後面可以接「當直接受詞的 that 子句」（a that-clause acting as the direct object）出處；當補語，英國文化協會的例子是 The problem was that I had no money.出處；放在介系詞後面，劍橋的例子是 The police seemed mainly interested in whether there were any locks on the windows.出處。

名詞子句常見的開頭有三種，英國文化協會在轉述動詞那頁把它們並列：that 子句（He said that I had to see a doctor.）、wh- 子句（She explained what we had to do.）、if 子句（He asked if I was ready.）。出處

  • that：說一件事（「……這件事」）。We believe that the price is fair.
  • whether／if：說「是不是……」。I don't know whether the price is fair.
  • 疑問詞（what、who、where、when、why、how、which）：說「什麼、誰、哪裡……」。I don't know what the price is.

名詞子句＝一整句話塞進名詞的位置：主詞、受詞、補語、介系詞後面。

在 What the client wants is a lower price. 這句裡，名詞子句是哪一段？

  • a lower price
  • What the client wants
  • the client wants
  • is a lower price

What the client wants（客戶要的東西）整段當句子的主詞，動詞是 is。少了 What，the client wants 就沒辦法當主詞。

在 The problem is that the budget is too small. 這句裡，that 子句在句子裡當什麼？

  • 主詞
  • 受詞
  • 介系詞後面的名詞
  • 補語

that the budget is too small 接在 is 後面，說明 The problem 是什麼，是補語。主詞是 The problem。

在 We believe the new system will save time. 這句裡，believe 的受詞是哪一段？

  • the new system
  • will save time
  • the new system will save time
  • time

我們相信的是「新系統會省時間」這整件事，believe 後面省略了 that，受詞是整個子句 the new system will save time。

在 We talked about whether we should hire a contractor. 這句裡，名詞子句站在哪個位置？

  • 介系詞後面
  • 主詞
  • 補語
  • 形容詞後面

whether we should hire a contractor 放在介系詞 about 後面，當 about 的受詞。

We are still discussing ______ we should hire an outside contractor.

  • that
  • what
  • whether
  • which

討論的是「要不要找外包」，是一件還沒決定的事，用 whether。what 放進去，後面 hire 的受詞 an outside contractor 已經有了，what 沒有位置；which 後面要接名詞或指前面的東西。

說一件事，……這件事

that 子句：動詞、形容詞、名詞後面，以及 It…that

劍橋：「that 當連接詞，把動詞、形容詞或名詞和後面的子句連起來」（We use that as a conjunction to link a verb, adjective or noun with the following clause.）。出處 that 在這裡只是連接詞，翻譯時常常不用翻出來。

動詞＋that 子句

常接 that 子句的動詞有兩類：轉述的動詞（say、tell、admit……）和講想法的動詞（believe、think、know、hope……）（Verbs commonly followed by that include reporting verbs (say, tell, admit, etc.) and mental process verbs (believe, think, know, hope, etc.)）。出處 劍橋在動詞句型頁列出的還有 agree、announce、confirm、explain、mention、notice、promise、realise、suggest、understand 等。出處

that 可以省略：這些動詞後面的 that「常常省略，尤其是非正式的口語」，在 guess、think、hope、reckon 後面特別常見（We often leave out that after these verbs, especially in informal speaking. … This is especially common after guess, think, hope and reckon）。出處 英國文化協會的例子：He said that I had to see a doctor. 也可以說 He said I had to see a doctor.。出處

tell、inform 後面要先接「人」：劍橋說有些轉述動詞「可以先接間接受詞（聽的人），再接當直接受詞的 that 子句」（Some verbs (generally those connected with reporting) can be followed by an indirect object … plus a that-clause acting as the direct object），列出的有 advise、assure、convince、inform、persuade、promise、remind、tell、warn；其中 tell 劍橋直接示範不能少掉人：He told us that it would take a long time.，不說 He told that it would take a long time.。出處 牛津學習者辭典列出的 inform 句型也都先接人，例如 inform somebody that…：I am pleased to inform you that you have won first prize in this month's competition.。出處 清單裡其他動詞不一定要接人，例如 warn 也能直接接 that 子句（牛津學習者辭典的例句：Aid agencies have repeatedly warned that a humanitarian catastrophe is imminent.）出處。對照：say 後面不直接接人，劍橋的同一頁把 say 列在直接接 that 子句的那一組。

形容詞＋that 子句、名詞＋that 子句

be＋形容詞＋that 子句表達意見和感受，常見的形容詞有 sure、certain、right、important、afraid、pleased、sorry、surprised、worried，而且「省略 that 意思不變」（We can omit that with no change in meaning）：I'm sure (that) you'll know a lot of people there.。名詞後面也能接 that 子句，常見的有 belief、fact、hope、idea、possibility、suggestion、statement、claim：Dutch police are investigating the possibility that a bomb was planted on the jet.。出處

that 子句當補語、當主詞

放在 be 後面當補語：英國文化協會說有些名詞後面接 be 再接 that 子句（We use some nouns with the verb be followed by a that clause），例子 The problem was that I had no money.、The obvious explanation is that he simply forgot.，常見的名詞有 answer、argument、belief、claim、explanation、hope、idea、rule、thing。出處

that 子句也能放句首當主詞，但比較正式；一般會把它移到句尾，前面放一個 It 佔住主詞的位置。劍橋：It's good that she's doing more exercise.（或比較正式的 That she's doing more exercise is good.），這個 it 是「虛主詞」，真正的主詞是後面的子句（We also call this use of it a 'dummy' subject, since the real subject is another part of the sentence）。出處 被動語態也常用：It was decided that we should all swim across the lake before breakfast.。出處

That＋子句＋is＋形容詞（正式）＝ It is＋形容詞＋that＋子句（一般）。

職場例句：

  • The supplier has confirmed that the parts will arrive on Monday.（供應商確認零件週一會到。）
  • I think we should postpone the launch.（我覺得應該延後上市。）think 後面省略 that。
  • Ms. Lin told us that the meeting had been moved to Thursday.（林小姐告訴我們會議改到週四。）
  • We are pleased that the project finished ahead of schedule.（很高興專案提前完成。）
  • There is a possibility that the launch will be delayed.（上市有可能延後。）
  • It is clear that the project needs more funding.（很明顯，這個專案需要更多經費。）
  • The main concern is that the costs keep rising.（最大的顧慮是成本一直上升。）

The supplier has confirmed ______ the parts will arrive on Monday.

  • that
  • what
  • which
  • who

confirm 後面接 that 子句說「確認了這件事」，that 後面 the parts will arrive 是完整的句子。what 要在子句裡當主詞或受詞，這裡沒有缺；which、who 前面要有先行詞。

Ms. Lin told ______ the meeting had been moved to Thursday.

  • that
  • to us that
  • us that
  • us what

tell 後面要先接聽的人，再接 that 子句：told us that。劍橋把 told that 列為錯誤；tell 不加 to；what 在這裡沒有位置，因為 the meeting had been moved 是完整的句子。

It is clear ______ we need to hire more staff before the busy season.

  • what
  • which
  • it
  • that

It is＋形容詞＋that 子句，It 是虛主詞，真正的主詞是 that 子句。we need to hire more staff 是完整的句子，用 that。

______ the factory will close next year is now certain.

  • What
  • That
  • It
  • Which

句首的子句當主詞，說「工廠明年會關」這件事，用 That（比較正式的寫法，一般說 It is now certain that…）。What 放進去，the factory will close 沒有缺東西；It 後面不能直接接完整的句子再接 is。

下面哪一句是對的？

  • He told that the deadline had passed.
  • He said us the deadline had passed.
  • He told us the deadline had passed.
  • He told to us the deadline had passed.

tell 後面先接人再接子句（that 可省略）：He told us the deadline had passed。say 後面不直接接人，tell 後面不加 to。

The main problem is ______ we don't have enough storage space.

  • what
  • which
  • that
  • this

is 後面的補語是「我們儲存空間不夠」這件事，後面是完整的句子，用 that。what、which 在這裡沒有位置；this 後面不能直接接一整個句子。

Are you certain ______ the invoice was sent to the correct address?

  • what
  • that
  • who
  • which

certain（確定的）是形容詞，後面接 that 子句說確定的內容。the invoice was sent… 是完整的句子；who、which 前面要有先行詞，what 沒有位置。

There is a strong possibility ______ the product launch will be delayed.

  • what
  • of
  • which
  • that

possibility 是劍橋列出的「名詞＋that 子句」常見名詞，that 子句說明可能性的內容。of 是介系詞，後面不能直接接 the product launch will be delayed 這種完整的句子；which 要在子句裡缺一個位置，這裡沒有缺。

是不是，whether 與 if

whether／if 子句：什麼時候只能用 whether

把一個「是不是」的問題（回答 yes 或 no 的問題）放進句子裡，用 whether 或 if 開頭。劍橋：「在間接的是非問句和有 or 的問句裡用 whether，不能省略 whether（或 if）」（We use whether in indirect yes-no questions and questions with or. We can't leave out whether (or if)），例如 They asked me whether (if) I was tired.，不說 They asked me I was tired.。出處

兩個都能用的時候，「if 比 whether 常見」（If is more common than whether），比較正式的場合偏好 whether（We often prefer whether in more formal contexts）。出處

只能用 whether 的四種情況

  • 後面直接接 to 不定詞：「to 不定詞前面用 whether，不用 if」（We use whether, not if, before to-infinitives）：I don't know whether to buy the blue one or the red one.，不說 if to buy。出處
  • 介系詞後面：「介系詞後面用 whether，不用 if」（We use whether, not if, after prepositions）：The police seemed mainly interested in whether there were any locks on the windows.，不說 interested in if。出處
  • 緊接著 or not：「or not 直接接在後面時用 whether」（We use whether, not if, directly before or not）：Can you tell me whether or not you're interested in the job.，不說 if or not。or not 放句尾的話，if 和 whether 都可以：I called Bill to find out if he really did go to Afghanistan or not.。出處
  • 句首當主詞：美國之音：「我們不會說 If he wins is not my concern。if 不當句子主詞的開頭；if 放在句首時，是在講條件，不是主詞的一部分」（We would not say, "If he wins is not my concern." The word "if" does not begin sentence subjects. When "if" begins a sentence, it introduces a conditional and is not part of the subject.）。出處

to 不定詞前、介系詞後、緊接 or not、句首當主詞 → 只能用 whether。

whether 也能帶出「不管是不是……」的意思（Whether you like it or not, you're going to have to look after your sister.）出處；這種 whether 子句不是名詞子句，而是修飾整句話。配對的 whether…or 在〈連接詞與連接副詞〉那課。

  • what

後面直接接 to 不定詞（to renew），只能用 whether，不用 if。that 不接 to 不定詞；what 放進去，renew 的受詞 the contract 已經有了。

The committee discussed ______ or not the budget should be increased.

  • if
  • that
  • what
  • whether

or not 緊接在後面，只能用 whether：whether or not。if or not 是劍橋列出的錯誤。

The client's final decision depends on ______ we can lower the price.

  • whether
  • if
  • that
  • what

介系詞 on 後面，用 whether 不用 if。that 子句不直接放在 on 後面；what 放進去，lower 的受詞 the price 已經有了。

______ the product will sell well in Europe is still unclear.

  • If
  • What
  • Whether
  • Which

句首的子句當主詞（動詞是 is），說「會不會賣得好」，用 Whether。If 放句首是在講條件，不能當主詞的開頭；What、Which 在子句裡沒有位置。

Please ask the hotel ______ the conference room is available on Friday.

  • that
  • if
  • what
  • which

要問飯店「有沒有空」，是一個是非問句放進句子裡，用 if（也可以用 whether）。ask 後面不接 that 子句來問問題；what、which 在子句裡沒有位置。

I'm not sure ______ the shipment has left the warehouse or not.

  • that
  • what
  • which
  • if

or not 放在句尾，if 可以用（whether 也可以）。that 子句是確定的事，不能配「or not」這種兩個可能。

下面哪一句是錯的？

  • We haven't decided whether or not to hire him.
  • We haven't decided whether to hire him.
  • We haven't decided if or not to hire him.
  • We haven't decided if we will hire him.

if 不能緊接 or not，也不能接 to 不定詞，所以 if or not to hire 錯兩次。if we will hire him 後面是完整子句，可以；另外兩句用 whether，都對。

The manager asked ______ the report would be ready by Monday.

  • that
  • what
  • whether
  • which

經理問的是「週一前會不會好」，是非問句放進句子，用 whether（或 if）。what、which 在子句裡沒有位置。

問句放進句子裡，語序改回直述句

疑問詞子句（間接問句）：Do you know where the office is?

把一個用 what、who、where、when、why、how 開頭的問句放進另一個句子，就是間接問句（indirect question）。重點只有一個：間接問句的語序跟直述句（一般陳述的句子）一樣，主詞在動詞前面，不倒裝。劍橋：「間接問句的語序跟直述句一樣（主詞＋動詞），不是問句的語序」（The word order in indirect reports of wh-questions is the same as statement word order (subject + verb), not question word order），例如 I asked her where the bus station was.（原本的問句是 Where is the bus station?）。出處

英國文化協會講得更直接：間接說法裡，「把問句的結構（例如 Do you like）改成直述句的結構（例如 I like）」（we change the question structure (e.g. Do you like) to a statement structure (e.g. I like)），例子 'What time does the train leave?' → He asked me what time the train left.。出處

改寫三步驟

  • 疑問詞留在最前面（where、what time、how much 整組一起搬）。
  • 主詞搬回動詞前面：where is the office → where the office is；when will the parts arrive → when the parts will arrive。
  • 問句裡幫忙的 do／does／did 拿掉，時態改回動詞身上：how much does it cost → how much it costs；when did it happen → when it happened（劍橋的例子：I asked her when it had happened，原句 When did it happen?）。出處
直接問句間接問句（放進句子裡）
Where is the meeting room?Could you tell me where the meeting room is?
How much does the software cost?Do you know how much the software costs?
When will the parts arrive?I'd like to know when the parts will arrive.
Why did the order ship late?Please explain why the order shipped late.
Who approved the budget?I don't know who approved the budget.（who 本身是主詞，語序不變）

疑問詞本身就是主詞時，語序不用改：劍橋：who、what 帶出的間接問句裡，疑問詞可以是子句的主詞或受詞，例如 I asked them who came to meet them at the airport.（who 是 came 的主詞；原句 Who came to meet you at the airport?）。出處

問號跟著整句走：單純轉述別人問了什麼，句尾「不用問號」（We don't use a question mark when reporting wh-questions）出處；但整句本身是在問人，就照樣用問號，例如劍橋的 Can you tell me where she left the keys?出處、Do you know how I can get to the bus station?出處。職場上用 Could you tell me…?、Do you know…? 問路、問資訊，就是間接問句最常見的用途。

疑問詞＋to 不定詞：講「該怎麼做、該去哪裡」時，疑問詞後面可以直接接 to 不定詞：英國文化協會的 I didn't know where to go.出處、劍橋的 I can't decide what to buy Liz for her birthday.出處。

when 後面可以用 will：〈完成進行式、未來的各種說法與時態總整理〉講過，when 當「在……的時候」時，子句用現在式代替 will；但 when 當疑問詞（「什麼時候」）放進間接問句時，後面可以用 will，例如 I wonder when the new computers will arrive.。出處 這課的間接問句都是這種情況。

間接問句：疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞（不倒裝、不加 do）。Do you know where the office is?

劍橋整理直接問句的常見錯誤時，強調只有助動詞和情態助動詞能放在主詞前面（We can only use auxiliary and modal verbs, not main verbs, before the subject），例如不說 Where was bought the painting?。出處 間接問句連助動詞都不搬到前面，所以 where is the office、where does the office 這種形狀放進句子裡都是錯的。

Excuse me, could you tell me where ______?

  • the nearest bank is
  • is the nearest bank
  • the nearest bank
  • does the nearest bank

間接問句用直述句語序：where＋主詞 the nearest bank＋動詞 is。is the nearest bank 是直接問句的語序；另外兩個少了動詞或動詞不對。

Do you know how much ______?

  • does the new software cost
  • costs the new software
  • the new software costs
  • the new software to cost

直接問句 How much does the software cost? 放進句子，拿掉 does，-s 回到動詞身上：how much the new software costs。to cost 沒有時態，不能當子句的動詞。

I wonder why ______ yet.

  • hasn't the order shipped
  • didn't the order ship
  • the order not shipped
  • the order hasn't shipped

why 後面用直述句語序：主詞 the order＋hasn't shipped。yet 配現在完成式。前兩個是直接問句的倒裝；the order not shipped 少了助動詞。

Please let me know when ______ available for a short call.

  • are you
  • you are
  • you
  • be you

when 在這裡是疑問詞（「什麼時候有空」），放進句子用直述句語序：you are。are you 是問句語序。

Can you tell me what time ______ tomorrow?

  • does the seminar start
  • starts the seminar
  • the seminar start
  • the seminar starts

what time 整組放前面，後面用直述句語序：the seminar starts（時刻表用現在式）。does 要拿掉，-s 回到 start 身上。

The receptionist asked the visitor who ______.

  • he wanted to see
  • did he want to see
  • wanted he to see
  • does he want to see

原句 Who do you want to see? 的 who 是 see 的受詞；改成間接問句要拿掉 do、主詞放回動詞前：who he wanted to see（主要子句是過去式，跟著用過去式）。did he want、does he want 都還留著問句的助動詞和倒裝；wanted he 把動詞搬到主詞前面，也是問句語序。

Nobody knows who ______ the large meeting room for this afternoon.

  • booking
  • was booked
  • booked
  • has book

who 本身是主詞（「誰訂了會議室」），語序不用改：who booked。booking 沒有時態；was booked 是被動，後面卻還有受詞 the large meeting room；has book 少了過去分詞。

I'm not sure what ______ at the next board meeting.

  • should we discuss
  • we should discuss
  • do we discuss
  • shall we discuss

間接問句：what＋主詞 we＋should discuss。should we、do we、shall we 都把助動詞放在主詞前面，是直接問句的語序。

Could you explain how ______ the new expense system?

  • using
  • use
  • to use
  • used

疑問詞＋to 不定詞：how to use（怎麼用）。how 後面直接接 -ing、原形或過去分詞都不成話。

下面哪一句是對的？

  • Do you know where is the elevator?
  • Do you know where the elevator is?
  • Do you know where the elevator?
  • Do you know where does the elevator?

Do you know 後面的間接問句用直述句語序：where the elevator is。整句是在問對方，所以句尾還是問號。

The customer wanted to know when ______.

  • would his refund be processed
  • was his refund processed
  • his refund would be processed
  • will his refund be processed

when 是疑問詞，後面用直述句語序：主詞 his refund＋would be processed。would his refund、was his refund、will his refund 都把助動詞搬到主詞前面，是直接問句的語序。

Can you tell me which gate ______ from?

  • does the flight to Tokyo leave
  • the flight to Tokyo leaves
  • leaves the flight to Tokyo
  • the flight to Tokyo leaving

which gate 整組放前面，後面接直述句：the flight to Tokyo leaves。介系詞 from 留在句尾。the flight to Tokyo leaving 沒有時態。

We haven't decided where ______ this year's company party.

  • holding
  • to hold
  • hold
  • held

還沒決定「要在哪裡辦」，主詞跟主要子句一樣是 we，用疑問詞＋to 不定詞：where to hold。

I'd like to know why ______ so long last week.

  • did the repair take
  • took the repair
  • the repair taking
  • the repair took

why＋主詞 the repair＋took（last week 用過去式）。did 要拿掉、時態回到 take 身上變 took。

what 自己就是名詞

what 對 that：what＝the thing(s) that

劍橋：「what 可以當代名詞，意思是『……的東西』」（We can use what as a pronoun to mean 'the thing(s) that'），例如 What we need to do is make a list of useful phone numbers.（我們需要做的是……）、I haven't got many Beatles CDs, but you can borrow what I have.。出處

what 跟 that 最大的差別：what 自己在子句裡當主詞或受詞，what we need 的 what 是 need 的受詞，What happened 的 What 是 happened 的主詞；that 只是連接詞（劍橋：We use that as a conjunction to link a verb, adjective or noun with the following clause出處），自己不在子句裡當任何角色，所以後面跟的是一個完整的句子。

句子子句裡缺什麼用
______ the client needs is a faster delivery.needs 少了受詞What
______ happened at the meeting surprised everyone.happened 少了主詞What
We know ______ the client needs a faster delivery.不缺（the client needs a faster delivery 是完整的句子）that
It is clear ______ the project needs more time.不缺that

做題時的看法：空格直接接一個子句時，子句缺主詞或受詞 → what；子句是完整的句子 → that。

這是做題時看空格的方法，不是文法書上的規則：what 還有別的用法，例如接名詞的 what time the train leaves（what 在這裡修飾 time），這時後面的子句看起來也是完整的。

另外兩個限制：

  • what 前面不能有先行詞（被說明的名詞）：「what 不當關係代名詞，要用 which」（We don't use what as a relative pronoun. We use which），不說 the book what the lecturer mentioned。出處 先行詞後面該用 which、who 還是 that，見〈關係詞〉。
  • 接 that 子句的字後面不能換成 what：劍橋：I am very happy that you can come and visit us.，不說 happy what you can come（We don't use what after words which take a that-clause）。出處

職場例句：

  • What the client needs is a faster delivery schedule.（客戶需要的是更快的出貨時程。）
  • Please read what I sent you yesterday before the meeting.（開會前請先看我昨天寄給你的東西。）
  • The new manager explained what we had to do.（新經理說明了我們要做的事。）
  • The survey shows that most customers prefer online payment.（調查顯示大多數客戶偏好線上付款。）
  • What surprised everyone was the size of the order.（讓大家驚訝的是訂單的量。）

______ the client needs is a faster delivery schedule.

  • That
  • Which
  • What
  • It

needs 少了受詞，What（＝the thing that）補上，整段 What the client needs 當主詞。That 後面要完整的句子；Which 前面要有先行詞；It 後面不能直接接 the client needs 再接 is。

We were pleased ______ the project finished ahead of schedule.

  • that
  • what
  • which
  • whether

pleased 是接 that 子句的形容詞，後面 the project finished ahead of schedule 是完整的句子，用 that。劍橋特別提醒這種字後面不能換成 what。

Please read ______ I sent you yesterday before the meeting.

  • that
  • what
  • which
  • whether

sent 少了「寄的東西」，而 read 前面也沒有先行詞，用 what（＝the thing that）。which 前面要有名詞；that 帶出的子句要完整，這裡缺受詞。

The new manager explained ______ we had to do during the audit.

  • that
  • which
  • it
  • what

do 少了受詞（要做的事），用 what。that 後面要完整的句子，we had to do 缺了受詞。

This is the report ______ the auditors asked for last week.

  • which
  • what
  • whether
  • where

前面有先行詞 the report，要用關係代名詞 which（或 that），what 不能接在先行詞後面。where 指地點，而且 for 後面缺受詞，where 補不了。

______ surprised everyone was the size of the first order.

  • That
  • What
  • It
  • Which

surprised 少了主詞，What 補上：What surprised everyone（讓大家驚訝的事）整段當主詞，動詞是 was。

The latest survey shows ______ most customers prefer online payment.

  • what
  • which
  • that
  • whose

most customers prefer online payment 是完整的句子，用 that 帶出 shows 的受詞。

I don't understand ______ you mean by "flexible pricing."

  • that
  • whether
  • which
  • what

mean 少了受詞（你指的東西），用 what。that、whether 後面都要完整的句子。

下面哪一句是對的？

  • We will review what you submitted.
  • We will review the documents what you submitted.
  • We will review that you submitted.
  • We will review which you submitted.

submitted 少了受詞、前面也沒有先行詞，用 what。有先行詞 the documents 時要用 which 或 that，不能用 what；that、which 單獨放在 review 後面都不對。

要求、建議，後面用原形

suggest、require、insist、It is important that：that 子句用原形

要求、建議、堅持這一類動詞後面的 that 子句，動詞常用原形（字典上的樣子，不加 -s、不改過去式）。英國文化協會：「有些轉述動詞後面可以接 that＋原形動詞。這部分的動詞是假設語氣（subjunctive），沒有時態」（Some reporting verbs can also be followed by that + the base form of the verb. The verb in this part of the sentence is in the subjunctive and has no tense.），例子 We suggest that she read the documents carefully before signing.、Steiner demanded that the government investigate the issue.。出處

注意兩個例子：主詞是 she 卻用 read（不是 reads），主要動詞 demanded 是過去式、子句卻用 investigate（不是 investigated）——這就是「沒有時態」的意思。英文文法把這種原形也歸在假設語氣（subjunctive）底下；用 if 講「如果……就……」的條件句在〈假設語氣〉那課。

哪些動詞：「轉述動詞表達『想要某件事發生』時，可以接這種原形」，包括 advise、ask、command、demand、insist、order、propose、recommend、request、suggest、urge（Reporting verbs can be followed by the subjunctive when they express something that is wanted. These verbs include advise, ask, command, demand, insist, order, propose, recommend, request, suggest and urge.）。出處 這份清單沒有 require，但 require（規定、要求）也一樣：牛津學習者辭典的例子 The law requires that everyone wear a seat belt.、The situation required that he be present.，英式也可以說 The situation required that he should be present.。出處

  • be 動詞用 be，被動用 be＋過去分詞：牛津學習者辭典的例子 The UN has demanded that all troops be withdrawn.（所有部隊「被撤出」）。出處
  • It is important that…也用原形：牛津：It is important that he attend every day.。出處 同理，essential、necessary、vital 這類「很重要、必要」的形容詞後面的 that 子句也常見原形。
  • 英式英文也用 should＋原形：牛津在同一批條目都標了英式說法：(British English) It is important that he should attend every day.、(British English also) He insists that she should come.、(British English also) I recommend that he should see a lawyer.。出處出處出處 劍橋也說 suggest 是過去式時，that 子句可以用 should：Her doctor suggested that she should reduce her working hours and take more exercise.。出處 所以原形和 should＋原形兩種都對。

suggest／recommend／require／request／insist／demand＋that＋主詞＋原形（be＋過去分詞）。

不是「要求」的時候，照一般時態

英國文化協會：「轉述動詞跟『想要』無關時，後面的 that 子句可以用各種時態，不需要用原形」（When reporting verbs do not relate to a desire, they can be followed by a that clause in a variety of tenses, and the subjunctive is not necessary.），這一組也列了 insist。出處 insist 有兩個意思：「要求某件事發生」，和「清楚說出某件事是真的，尤其別人不信的時候」（to state clearly that something is true, especially when other people do not believe you）。出處 第二種是「堅稱」，照一般時態：The supplier insisted that the goods were in perfect condition when they left the factory.（供應商堅稱貨出廠時完好無缺。）

suggest、recommend 的其他句型

劍橋：suggest 後面不接「人＋to 不定詞」，也不直接接 to 不定詞（We don't use suggest + indirect object + to-infinitive；We don't use suggest + to-infinitive），要說 He suggested that I should apply for a job…、Dora suggested waiting until the news had been announced officially.。出處 recommend 也一樣，不說 recommend to do，說 I recommend taking an umbrella 或 I recommend you take an umbrella。出處 動詞後面接 -ing 還是 to 不定詞，整理在〈不定詞與動名詞〉。

  • Our lawyer recommended that we review the contract carefully.（律師建議我們仔細看過合約。）

Our lawyer recommended that we ______ the contract carefully before signing it.

四種名詞子句混在一起

綜合練習

做題順序：先看空格後面是不是一個子句；再看那個子句缺不缺主詞或受詞（缺 → what 或其他疑問詞）、是不是「是不是」的問題（→ whether／if）、是不是陳述一件事（→ that）；最後檢查子句的語序是不是直述句。

Could you tell me ______ the quarterly report is due?

  • who
  • what
  • when
  • which

要問的是「什麼時候到期」，用疑問詞 when，後面直述句語序 the quarterly report is due。who、what、which 在這個子句裡都沒有位置：主詞 the quarterly report 已經有了，is due 後面也不缺受詞。

______ we need right now is more time to test the software.

  • That
  • What
  • Whether
  • If

need 少了受詞，What 補上：What we need（我們需要的東西）當主詞。Whether、If 後面要完整的句子，而且 If 不當主詞的開頭。

The client asked ______ we could deliver the goods by Friday.

  • what
  • that
  • which
  • if

客戶問「能不能週五前交貨」，是非問句放進句子，用 if（或 whether）。what、which 在子句裡沒有位置。

It is recommended that each passenger ______ at the gate 30 minutes before departure.

  • arriving
  • arrive
  • to arrive
  • arrived

It is recommended that＋主詞＋原形：arrive（主詞 each passenger 也不加 -s）。arrived 是過去式，跟「建議旅客要怎麼做」對不上；-ing、to 不定詞不能當子句的動詞。

I'm not sure who ______ the presentation at tomorrow's meeting.

  • will he give
  • giving
  • will give
  • gave

who 是主詞（「誰會報告」），語序不變：who will give。will he give 多塞了一個主詞，而且是問句語序；giving 沒有時態；gave 是過去式，跟 tomorrow's meeting 衝突。

We are interested in ______ your company offers discounts for bulk orders.

  • whether
  • if
  • that
  • what

介系詞 in 後面，「是不是有折扣」用 whether，不用 if。what 放進去，offers 的受詞 discounts 已經有了。

重點整理

  • 名詞子句是一整個子句當名詞用：當主詞、受詞、補語，或放在介系詞後面；開頭是 that、whether／if 或疑問詞。
  • that 子句說一件事；think、say、hope 和 sure、afraid 後面的 that 常省略；tell、inform 後面要先接人；that 子句當主詞時一般改成 It is…that…。
  • whether／if 說「是不是」；to 不定詞前、介系詞後、緊接 or not、句首當主詞，只能用 whether。
  • 間接問句用直述句語序：疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞，拿掉 do／does／did；疑問詞本身是主詞時語序不變；疑問詞＋to 不定詞（how to use）。
  • what＝the thing(s) that，自己在子句裡當主詞或受詞，前面不能有先行詞；that 只是連接詞，後面接完整的句子。
  • suggest、recommend、request、require、insist、demand 表示要求，以及 It is important／essential that，子句用原形（被動用 be＋過去分詞），英式也用 should＋原形；insist 當「堅稱」時照一般時態。
