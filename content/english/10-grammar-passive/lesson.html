讀完這課你會

  • 用 be＋過去分詞寫被動句
  • 寫出各時態的被動，知道哪些動詞不能改
  • 處理授與動詞與 make 的被動
  • 判斷句子填空該用主動還是被動

被動的形狀，be＋過去分詞

被動語態：把「被做的那一方」放到句首

一般的句子是主動語態：主詞是做事的人。The manager approved the budget.（經理核准了預算）的主詞 The manager 就是做「核准」的人。被動語態反過來，把被做的那一方（預算）搬到句首當主詞：The budget was approved by the manager.（預算被經理核准了）。

劍橋的說法：主動語態是一般的字序，主詞是做這個動作的人；被動語態裡，被做的那一方變成句子的主題，做事的人可以省略，也可以放在 by 後面（In the passive, the person or thing that the action was done to becomes the topic or theme. We can leave out the 'doer' or agent, or we can place the 'doer' in a prepositional phrase (by + 'doer')）。出處

被動語態＝be 動詞＋過去分詞；時態由 be 決定，過去分詞不變

英國文化協會：被動用 be 動詞加過去分詞組成，句子從原本的受詞開始（We make the passive using the verb be + past participle. We start the sentence with the object.）。出處 過去分詞就是動詞三態的第三個（approve → approved → approved、write → wrote → written），不規則的寫法在〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉的表裡。

主動改被動的三個步驟

  • 步驟一：原本的受詞搬到句首當主詞。所以主動句一定要有受詞，才改得成被動（下面「要有受詞才能改被動」那段會再講）。
  • 步驟二：動詞改成 be＋過去分詞。be 的時態跟原本的動詞一樣（原句是過去式，be 就用 was／were），單複數配新的主詞。
  • 步驟三：原本的主詞放到 by 後面，或直接省略。代名詞放到 by 後面要改成受格：by him、by them。

by 片語什麼時候可以省略

劍橋：做事的人重要時，用 by 帶出來（We use this structure when the agent is important）；做事的人不重要、不知道或很明顯時，就不寫，而且不寫的被動句非常常見（Passive structures without an agent are very common. We use these structures when an agent is not important, or is unknown or obvious）。出處 英國文化協會也提到，被動常用在不想說是誰做的、想讓句子從最重要的資訊開始，以及比較正式的書面文字（We often use the passive ... in more formal or scientific writing.）。出處 所以職場公告、規章、電子郵件裡常見被動。

The quarterly report ______ by the finance team every March.

  • is prepared
  • prepares
  • is preparing
  • prepare

報告是「被準備」的，空格後面又接 by the finance team，要用被動 is prepared。prepares、is preparing 是主動，後面得接受詞；prepare 主詞單數也沒加 s。

All visitors are ______ to wear a badge inside the factory.

  • require
  • requiring
  • required
  • requires

空格前面已經有 are，訪客是「被要求」的一方，are 後面接過去分詞 required。

主動句 The manager approved the budget. 改成被動，哪一句正確？

  • The budget approved by the manager.
  • The budget is approved by the manager.
  • The manager was approved the budget.
  • The budget was approved by the manager.

受詞 the budget 搬到句首、動詞改成 be＋過去分詞、原主詞放到 by 後面。原句是過去式，be 也要用過去式 was；is approved 把時態改掉了。The budget approved 少了 be。

Our office ______ every evening, so please take your belongings home before you leave.

  • cleans
  • is cleaned
  • is cleaning
  • cleaned

辦公室不會自己打掃，是「被打掃」；every evening 是習慣，用現在簡單式的被動 is cleaned。打掃的人很明顯，省略 by 片語。

The winners of the design contest ______ next Monday.

  • will announce
  • announced
  • will be announced
  • have announced

得獎者是「被宣布」的一方，而且 next Monday 是未來，用未來式的被動 will be announced。will announce 是主動，後面要接宣布的內容。

Your application ______ yesterday, and you will hear from us within a week.

  • received
  • receives
  • was receiving
  • was received

申請書是「被收到」的；yesterday 是過去，用 was received。其他三個都是主動，意思變成「申請書收到了某樣東西」。

在 The contract was signed by both parties. 這句裡，做「簽」這個動作的是誰？

  • both parties
  • The contract
  • was signed
  • by

被動句的主詞 The contract 是被簽的東西；做事的人放在 by 後面，是 both parties（雙方）。

The large meeting room ______ for the client presentation tomorrow morning.

  • has booked
  • has been booked
  • is booking
  • booked

會議室是「被預訂」的，用現在完成式的被動 has been booked（已經訂好了）。has booked、is booking、booked 都是主動，變成會議室去訂東西。

時態，只換 be

各時態的被動：be 換時態，過去分詞不動

英國文化協會：要換時態，只換 be 的形式，過去分詞保持不變（Only the form of be changes to make the tense. The past participle stays the same.）。出處 所以只要會〈時間軸怎麼看：簡單式三態〉、〈進行式三態〉、〈完成式三態：現在完成式對過去式〉裡 be 和 have 的變化，被動就會了。劍橋：被動的各時態用法跟主動一樣（We use passive forms of tenses in the same way as we use their active equivalents.）。出處

時態被動的形狀例句意思
現在簡單式am / is / are＋過去分詞The office is cleaned every evening.辦公室每晚有人打掃。
過去簡單式was / were＋過去分詞The order was shipped yesterday.訂單昨天寄出了。
未來（will）will be＋過去分詞The results will be announced on Friday.結果週五公布。
未來（be going to）am / is / are going to be＋過去分詞The old printers are going to be replaced.舊印表機會被換掉。
現在進行式am / is / are being＋過去分詞The east wing is being renovated.東側大樓正在整修。
過去進行式was / were being＋過去分詞The elevator was being repaired at 9 a.m.早上九點電梯正在修。
現在完成式have / has been＋過去分詞Three engineers have been hired since May.五月以來聘了三位工程師。
過去完成式had been＋過去分詞The samples had been tested before the client arrived.客戶到之前樣品已經測過了。
未來完成式will have been＋過去分詞The bridge will have been completed by 2028.到 2028 年橋會完工。
助動詞助動詞＋be＋過去分詞All reports must be submitted by Friday.所有報告必須在週五前交。

表中大部分的形狀，劍橋的被動形式表與英國文化協會的時態表都列了：

  • 現在進行式：is/are being + past participle
  • 現在完成式：has/have been + past participle
  • 未來：will be + past participle
  • 助動詞：can、will、might 加 be 再加過去分詞（Reservations can be accepted up to 24 hours before arrival.）。出處出處
  • be going to：英國文化協會的被動例子是 The doors are going to be locked at ten o'clock.。出處

其餘各列（例如未來完成式 will have been＋過去分詞）照「只換 be、過去分詞不變」同一個規則推出來。

完成進行式（has been being＋過去分詞）的被動很少用，劍橋建議改寫成主動：說 They have been renovating the house for almost a year.，不說 The house has been being renovated ...。出處

否定和疑問也只動 be（或助動詞）：The invoice hasn't been paid yet.（請款單還沒付）、Was the package delivered on time?（包裹準時送到了嗎？）、When will the new system be installed?（新系統什麼時候裝？）。

多益怎麼考：閱讀測驗第五部分「句子填空」共 30 題，每題一個句子少一個字或片語，從四個選項選最適合的。出處 被動題常見的出法是把同一個動詞的主動、被動、不同時態放在一起。解題順序建議：先看主詞是做事的還是被做的（決定主動或被動），再找時間線索（決定 be 用哪個時態）。

  • has renovated
  • is being renovated
  • renovates

大樓是「被整修」的；at the moment（此刻）用進行式，合起來是現在進行式的被動 is being renovated。

The factory in Kaohsiung ______ in 2019.

工廠是「被蓋」的；in 2019 是明確的過去時間，用過去簡單式的被動 was built。明確的過去時間不配現在完成式，所以不是 has been built。

Three new engineers ______ since January.

  • were hired
  • are hired
  • hired
  • have been hired

工程師是「被聘」的；since January 從過去算到現在，用現在完成式的被動 have been hired。

When the inspector arrived, the samples had already ______.

  • tested
  • been tested
  • being tested
  • be tested

樣品是「被測試」的，而且在檢查員到之前就測完了（過去完成式）。had 後面接 been＋過去分詞：had been tested。had already tested 是主動，變成樣品去測東西。

The new pricing policy ______ at next week's board meeting.

  • will discuss
  • was discussed
  • has been discussed
  • will be discussed

政策是「被討論」的；next week's 是未來，用 will be discussed。

While the elevator ______, employees had to use the stairs.

  • was being repaired
  • was repairing
  • is repaired
  • has repaired

電梯是「被修」的；後半句 had to 是過去，while 講過去一段正在進行的時間，用過去進行式的被動 was being repaired。

All expense reports must ______ by Friday.

  • submit
  • submitted
  • be submitted
  • be submitting

報告是「被交」的；助動詞 must 的被動是 must be＋過去分詞。must submit 是主動，後面要接受詞；must submitted 少了 be。

The signed documents are going to ______ to the client tomorrow.

  • send
  • be sent
  • sent
  • sending

文件是「被寄」的；be going to 的被動是 be going to be＋過去分詞：are going to be sent。

The results of the employee survey ______ yet.

  • haven't announced
  • aren't announcing
  • didn't announce
  • haven't been announced

這題看語態：結果是「被公布」的，四個選項裡只有 haven't been announced 是被動，其他三個都是主動，變成結果去公布東西。

A new security system ______ in the parking garage next month.

  • installed
  • is installing
  • will be installed
  • has been installed

系統是「被安裝」的，next month 是未來：will be installed。has been installed 是「已經裝好」，跟 next month 對不上。

誰能改被動，及物動詞

要有受詞才能改被動：happen、occur、arrive 不行

步驟一說「受詞搬到句首」，所以沒有受詞的動詞就改不了被動。〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉講過及物動詞（後面一定要接受詞的動詞）和不及物動詞（後面不接受詞的動詞）。

  • 及物動詞：才有主動和被動兩種形式。英國文化協會：Transitive verbs have both active and passive forms出處
  • 不及物動詞：劍橋說沒有受詞的動詞（不及物動詞）不能改成被動，例子是說 The parcel arrived in the post this morning.，不說 The parcel was arrived ...。出處
  • 典型錯誤：劍橋列的例子是 She died.，不說 She was died.。出處

職場句子常見的三個：

  • happen（發生）：劍橋列為最常當不及物用的動詞（Verbs which are most commonly used intransitively: arrive ... happen）。出處 說 What happened?，不說 What was happened?。
  • occur（發生，比 happen 正式）：劍橋辭典標為 [ I ]，也就是不及物（An accident involving over ten vehicles has occurred in the east-bound lane.）。出處 說 An error occurred.，不說 An error was occurred.。
  • arrive（抵達）：同一份清單裡的不及物動詞，要接地點得先加介系詞，例如 arrive at the airport。出處

另外兩種也要注意：連綴動詞（be、become、seem 這種後面接「說明主詞」的補語的動詞）不改被動（We don't form passive structures with verbs like be, become, seem），例如 She has finally become a doctor.；狀態動詞（〈進行式三態〉講過）通常也不用被動（We don't usually use the passive with some verbs that describe a state），劍橋的例子是說 They were having lunch.，不說 Lunch was being had.。出處

反過來，劍橋說被動可以用在及物動詞，也可以用在一部分「動詞＋介系詞」的句子（We can form passive structures with verbs that are followed by an object (transitive verbs) and some clauses where the verb is followed by a preposition），介系詞跟著動詞走：劍橋的例子 The holiday hasn't been paid for yet.（假期的錢還沒付）出處；英國文化協會的例子 The meeting was called off.（會議取消了）、He was looked after by his grandmother.。出處

The guests from Osaka ______ at the hotel shortly after 8 p.m.

  • are arrived
  • arrived

arrive 是不及物動詞，沒有被動，所以三個 be＋arrived 的選項都錯，用過去式 arrived。

What ______ to the shipment that was supposed to arrive on Monday?

  • was happened
  • has been happened
  • happened

happen 不及物，沒有被動。問過去的事用過去式 happened。

下面哪一個動詞可以改成被動語態？

  • approve
  • arrive
  • happen
  • occur

approve（核准）是及物動詞，要說核准「什麼」，所以有被動 be approved。其他三個都是不及物動詞。

The outdoor product launch ______ because of the typhoon warning.

  • called off
  • was called off
  • was calling off
  • has called off

發表會是「被取消」的；call off 改被動，off 跟著動詞留下：was called off。其他三個是主動，後面都少了受詞。

New employees ______ by a mentor during their first month.

  • are looked
  • look after
  • are looking after
  • are looked after

look after（照顧）改被動，after 不能丟：are looked after。are looked 少了 after，意思不通；另外兩個是主動，新進員工變成去照顧人。

Ms. Park ______ the head of the sales department last year.

  • became
  • was become
  • has been become
  • is became

become 是連綴動詞，後面的 the head of the sales department 是說明主詞的補語，不是受詞，不能改被動。last year 用過去式 became。

兩種特別的被動

授與動詞的兩種被動；make 改被動要補 to

授與動詞：人當主詞、東西當主詞都可以

授與動詞是後面可以接兩個受詞（人和東西）的動詞，例如 give、send、offer、show，也就是〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉的 S＋V＋O＋O。劍橋：有兩個受詞的動詞，兩個受詞都可以拿來當被動句的主詞，看要強調哪一個（When verbs have two objects, either object can be the theme or subject of the passive structure, depending on what we want to focus on），例子是 A present was given to each child. 和 Each child was given a present.。出處 英國文化協會也說，主動句裡的「人」（間接受詞）可以當被動句的主詞（We can use the indirect object as the subject of a passive verb），例如 She was sent a cheque for a thousand euros.。出處

主動人當主詞東西當主詞
We sent the client the invoice.The client was sent the invoice.The invoice was sent to the client.
They offered Ms. Lin a position.Ms. Lin was offered a position.A position was offered to Ms. Lin.
HR gave each new hire a laptop.Each new hire was given a laptop.A laptop was given to each new hire.

看兩個寫法的差別：人當主詞時，東西直接跟在過去分詞後面（was sent the invoice）；東西當主詞時，give、send、offer、show 這類動詞在人前面加 to（was sent to the client），跟劍橋例句 A present was given to each child 的寫法一樣。

make 改被動要補 to；let 不改被動

使役動詞是「叫別人做某件事」的動詞，例如 make（強迫）、let（讓）。劍橋：let 和 make 在主動句裡接不帶 to 的原形動詞（Let and make are followed by an infinitive without to in active voice sentences.）出處；但 make 改成被動時，一定要用帶 to 的不定詞（However, in the passive voice, we must use an infinitive with to），例子是 The people were made to wait outside while the committee reached its decision.。出處

  • 主動：The manager made us work overtime.（經理叫我們加班。）
  • 被動：We were made to work overtime.（我們被要求加班。）

let（允許）這個意思不用被動，劍橋說要改用 be allowed to：說 We weren't allowed to take photographs ...，不說 We weren't let (to) take photographs。出處

感官動詞（see、hear 這類看到、聽到的動詞）在主動句裡也接不帶 to 的原形：We saw their car drive past the house yesterday.。出處 改成被動時，原形前面要補 to：牛津學習者辭典 see 條目列了 somebody/something is seen to do something 這個句型（He was seen to enter the building about the time the crime was committed.）；同一個條目也收了接 -ing 的被動（She was seen running away from the scene of the crime.），所以 was seen leaving 也對。出處 例如：Mr. Ross was seen to leave the office at midnight.（有人看到羅斯先生半夜離開辦公室），也可以說 was seen leaving。

have＋東西＋過去分詞：請別人做

職場常見的另一個被動形狀：have＋東西＋過去分詞，表示「請別人替我們做某件事」。劍橋：用來講我們請人或交代人替我們做的事，重點在事情本身而不是誰做的（We use have + object + -ed form when we talk about someone doing something for us which we ask or instruct them to do.）。出處

  • We had the brochures translated into Japanese.（我們請人把型錄翻成日文。）
  • I'm having my laptop repaired this week.（我這週把筆電送修。）

Each new employee ______ a laptop on the first day.

  • will give
  • will be given
  • gives
  • is giving

新進員工是「被給」筆電的一方，人當主詞，東西 a laptop 直接接在過去分詞後面：will be given a laptop。主動的三個意思變成員工送出筆電。

主動句 We sent the client the invoice. 用「東西」當主詞改成被動，哪一句正確？

  • The invoice was sent the client.
  • The invoice sent to the client.
  • The invoice was sent to the client.
  • The client was sent to the invoice.

send 的被動以東西當主詞時，人前面加 to：was sent to the client。The invoice sent 少了 be；The client was sent to the invoice. 把人和東西的關係弄反了。

主動句 The company gave each winner a bonus. 用「人」當主詞改成被動，哪一句正確？

  • Each winner was given to a bonus.
  • Each winner gave a bonus.
  • Each winner was gave a bonus.
  • Each winner was given a bonus.

人當主詞，東西直接接在 was given 後面，不加 to。was gave 用錯了，be 後面要過去分詞 given。

The warehouse staff were made ______ late to finish the inventory count.

  • to stay
  • stay
  • staying
  • stayed

make 改被動，原形動詞前面要補 to：were made to stay。主動句才是 made them stay。

Interns ______ to use the executive parking lot.

  • are not let
  • do not let
  • are not allowed
  • are not letting

「被允許」的被動用 be allowed to；let 這個意思不用被動，所以沒有 are not let to。

We had the product brochures ______ into Japanese for the trade fair.

  • translate
  • translating
  • to translate
  • translated

型錄是請人「被翻譯」的，用 have＋東西＋過去分詞：had the brochures translated。

  • was offered
  • offered
  • was offering
  • has offered

林小姐是「被提供」職位、然後接受的一方：was offered a position（人當主詞，東西直接接後面）。主動的寫法變成她把職位給別人，跟「她接受了」對不上。

Mr. Ross was seen ______ the office at midnight.

  • leave
  • to leave
  • left
  • leaves

主動是 saw him leave；改成被動 was seen 後面接帶 to 的不定詞 to leave（接 -ing 的 leaving 也對，但不在選項裡）。was seen leave 少了 to。

多益常見，二選一與固定搭配

主動還是被動？看主詞、看空格後面有沒有受詞

選項裡同時有主動和被動時，兩個問題問完通常就有答案：

  • 主詞是做事的，還是被做的？報告、訂單、會議室、產品這類東西，通常是被做的一方。
  • 空格後面有沒有受詞？及物動詞的主動後面一定接受詞（〈詞性與句子骨架：五大句型與「這格填什麼詞性」〉）；空格後面直接是介系詞、時間詞或句號，又是及物動詞時，多半是被動。這是解題建議，例外是授與動詞的被動（was given a laptop）後面還會接一個東西。
例句主動還是被動看哪裡
Mr. Davis signed the contract this morning.主動主詞是人，後面有受詞 the contract
The contract was signed this morning.被動主詞是合約，後面直接接時間
The marketing team launched a new campaign.主動後面有受詞 a new campaign
A new campaign was launched last month.被動後面直接接時間
The conference room has been reserved for 2 p.m.被動會議室是被訂的
Our accountant has reviewed the figures.主動後面有受詞 the figures

be＋過去分詞＋介系詞或 to：要整組背

英國文化協會：形容詞配哪個介系詞沒有文法規則可循，最好一起背（There are no grammatical rules for which preposition is used with which adjective, so it's a good idea to try to learn them together.）。出處 另外有一批動詞很常用被動，後面接 to＋原形，英國文化協會列了 be supposed to、be expected to、be asked to、be told to、be scheduled to、be allowed to、be invited to、be ordered to（Some verbs which are very frequently used in the passive are followed by the to-infinitive）。出處

搭配意思例句出處
be satisfied with對……滿意Are you satisfied with the new supplier?出處
be involved in參與……Ms. Rivera is involved in the merger talks.出處
be based on以……為根據The new design is based on customer feedback.出處
be located in位於……Our headquarters is located in Taipei.出處
be required to被要求、必須……All staff are required to complete the training.出處
be expected to預計……；應該要……The shipment is expected to arrive on Thursday.出處
be scheduled to排定……The keynote speech is scheduled to begin at 9 a.m.出處
be asked to被請求……Participants are asked to bring a photo ID.出處
be supposed to照規定或約定應該……You are supposed to submit the form by noon.出處

表中的介系詞是劍橋辭典各條目與英國文化協會的例句寫法（Are you satisfied with the new arrangement?、I didn't want to get involved in the argument.、be based on sth、Our office is located in midtown Manhattan.、We're required to check your identification before letting you in.）。be involved 也可以接 with，同樣是「參與」（劍橋辭典 involve 條目：She's been involved with animal rights for many years.）出處；練習題裡不拿 with 當陷阱選項。

長得很像的，過去分詞當形容詞

interested 和 interesting：簡單提一下

過去分詞（-ed 形）和現在分詞（-ing 形）也能當形容詞，這時看起來像被動，其實是在說明主詞的樣子。劍橋：-ing 形容詞描述「造成的效果」，-ed 形容詞描述「人的感受」（-ing adjectives describe the effect ... -ed adjectives describe how a person feels）。出處 例如 We are interested in your proposal.（我們有興趣）對 Your proposal is interesting.（提案有意思）。完整的對照與練習在〈分詞與分詞構句〉。

We are very ______ with the results of the pilot project.

  • satisfying
  • satisfied
  • satisfaction
  • satisfies

主詞 We 是「感到滿意」的人，用 -ed 形 satisfied，接 with；satisfying 說的是讓人滿意的東西，satisfaction 是名詞，satisfies 是動詞。

Sales for the third quarter were ______, so the team is reviewing its strategy.

  • disappointed
  • disappoint
  • disappointment
  • disappointing

業績是「令人失望」的那一方，用 -ing 形 disappointing。

重點整理

  • 被動語態＝be＋過去分詞；主動改被動三步：受詞搬到句首、動詞改 be＋過去分詞（時態不變，單複數配新主詞）、原主詞放 by 後面或省略。
  • 做事的人不重要、不知道或很明顯時省略 by 片語；公告、規章、電子郵件常用沒有 by 的被動。
  • 時態只換 be：is done、was done、will be done、is being done、has been done、had been done、助動詞＋be done。
  • 要有受詞才能改被動（及物動詞，和一部分動詞＋介系詞）：happen、occur、arrive、die 和連綴動詞 become 都沒有被動；call off、look after 這類動詞片語可以，介系詞或副詞留著。
  • 授與動詞兩種被動：人當主詞（was given a laptop）、東西當主詞時，give、send、offer、show 這類在人前面加 to（was sent to the client）。make 改被動要補 to（be made to work）；let 改用 be allowed to。
  • 主動被動二選一：看主詞是做事的還是被做的、空格後面有沒有受詞。固定搭配整組背：be satisfied with、be involved in、be based on、be located in、be required / expected / scheduled / asked to。
  • -ed 形容詞說人的感受（interested），-ing 形容詞說東西帶來的效果（interesting）。
