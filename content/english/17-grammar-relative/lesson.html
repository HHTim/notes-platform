關係代名詞：先行詞是人用 who，是東西用 which，that 兩種都行

關係子句（relative clause）是放在名詞後面、說明「是哪一個」或補充資料的子句。劍橋：「關係子句提供某人或某物更多的資訊；用關係子句可以把兩個子句合在一起，不必重複資訊」（Relative clauses give us more information about someone or something. We can use relative clauses to combine clauses without repeating information.），例如 The couple posted a Christmas present to their daughter, who lives in South Africa.，不必再說一次 their daughter。出處 被關係子句說明的那個名詞叫先行詞（antecedent，「先出現的那個詞」）；帶出關係子句的 who、which、that 這些字叫關係代名詞（relative pronoun）。

The engineer who designed the warehouse is retiring.：先行詞 engineer，關係代名詞 who，關係子句 who designed the warehouse。

whom：受格，正式

劍橋：「whom 是 who 的受格，在正式文體或書面裡指人，而且這個人是動詞的受詞」（Whom is the object form of who. We use whom to refer to people in formal styles or in writing, when the person is the object of the verb.）。出處 但英國文化協會說現在一般都用 who（but nowadays we normally use who）：This is George, who you met at our house last year.。出處 所以在受詞的位置，who、whom、that 都對（劍橋的例子 The parents (whom/who/that) we interviewed…）。出處 主詞的位置就只能用 who，不能用 whom。

可以省略的關係代名詞

劍橋說在不正式的文體裡常省略關係代名詞（In informal styles, we often leave out the relative pronoun），而且「只有在限定的關係子句裡、關係代名詞是動詞的受詞時，才省略；當主詞的不省略，非限定的關係子句也不省略」（We only do this in defining relative clauses, and when the relative pronoun is the object of the verb. We don’t leave out the relative pronoun when it is the subject of the verb nor in non-defining relative clauses）。出處 英國文化協會給了一個好用的判斷法：關係代名詞「後面接的是主詞」通常可以省，「後面接的是動詞」通常不能省（we can usually leave out who, which or that if it is followed by a subject）。出處

  • The employee who answered the phone was very polite.（who 後面接動詞 answered，不能省。）

whose＋名詞：表示所有

介系詞放哪裡，以及地點、時間、原因

介系詞＋whom／which；關係副詞 where、when、why

介系詞放前面或放後面

劍橋：介系詞可以「直接放在關係代名詞前面（比較正式），或放在關係子句的最後（比較不正式）」（We can put the preposition immediately before the relative pronoun (more formal) or at the end of the relative clause (more informal).），例如 the community in which he grew up／the community which I grew up in；指人時是 There was only one person to whom the old man spoke.／…who the old man spoke to.。出處

  • 介系詞後面不接 who：用 who 時，「介系詞放在關係子句最後，不直接放在 who 前面」（We put the preposition at the end of the relative clause, and not immediately before who），劍橋的錯句是 the one on who I know I can rely。出處
  • 介系詞後面不接 that：「跟 which、whom、whose 不一樣，that 前面不能直接放介系詞」（Unlike which, whom and whose, we can’t use that with the preposition immediately before it）。出處

介系詞放前面：to whom（人）、in which（東西）；放後面：who…to、which／that…in。

關係副詞：where、when、why

劍橋：「在不正式的用語裡，常用 where、when、why 代替 at which、on which、for which 帶出限定的關係子句」（In informal language, we often use where, when or why to introduce defining relative clauses instead of at which, on which or for which）：I know a restaurant where the food is excellent.（＝at which）、There isn’t a day when I don’t feel rushed off my feet.（＝on which）、Do you know the reason why the shop is closed today?（＝for which）。出處 指時間不用 where：劍橋的錯句 another date where everyone can attend，要說 another date when everyone can attend。出處

where 對 which：看子句完不完整

英國文化協會說，which 加上介系詞可以指地點和時間，但「用 where 或 when 代替 which 加介系詞更常見」（In these cases it's more common to use where or when instead of which and the preposition）；但「which 沒有介系詞時，就不能換成 where 或 when」（However, when we use which without a preposition, we can't use where or when.）：City Park, where we used to go, …（＝which we used to go to），但 Centre Park, which we love, … 不能換成 where。出處

介系詞 to 直接放在前面、指人，用 whom。介系詞後面不直接接 who 或 that；which 不用在人。

The conference room in ______ we hold our weekly meetings is being painted.

  • where
  • that
  • which
  • what

介系詞 in 直接放在前面、指東西，用 which：in which。in where、in that、in what 都不成話。

This is the hotel ______ we stayed during the trade fair.

  • which
  • that
  • what
  • where

we stayed during the trade fair 是完整的子句（stay 不接受詞），指地點用 where（＝at which）。which、that 要在句尾補 at 才對。

The city ______ we visited last month holds a large trade fair every spring.

  • which
  • where
  • when
  • what

visit 後面要接受詞，we visited 少了受詞，所以用 which（受詞），不用 where。where 後面要接完整的子句。

That is the warehouse ______ the damaged goods are stored.

  • which
  • where
  • whose
  • what

the damaged goods are stored 是完整的被動句，不缺主詞也不缺受詞，指地點用 where。

Friday is the day ______ the new ordering system will go live.

  • where
  • which
  • when
  • what

先行詞 day 是時間，後面是完整的子句，用 when（＝on which）。指時間不用 where；which 要加 on。

Nobody has explained the reason ______ the shipment was returned.

  • why
  • which
  • where
  • what

先行詞 reason，後面是完整的子句，用 why（＝for which）。

I still remember the year ______ our company opened its first overseas branch.

  • where
  • which
  • what
  • when

先行詞 year 是時間，後面 our company opened its first overseas branch 是完整的子句，用 when。

The budget on ______ the whole project depends has finally been approved.

  • that
  • which
  • what
  • it

depend on something，介系詞 on 提到前面、指東西，用 on which。that、what 不放在介系詞後面；it 會讓句子多出一組主詞動詞卻沒有連接。

前面沒有先行詞的時候

what＝the thing that，不是關係代名詞

劍橋：「what 可以當代名詞，意思是『……的東西』」（We can use what as a pronoun to mean ‘the thing(s) that’），例如 What we need to do is make a list of useful phone numbers.；但「what 不當關係代名詞，要用 which」（We don’t use what as a relative pronoun. We use which）：This is the book which the lecturer mentioned.，不說 the book what…。出處

前面有名詞（先行詞）→ which／that；前面沒有名詞 → what（＝the thing that）。

劍橋在關係代名詞頁也提醒：「用 which 或 that，不用 what」（We use which or that, not what），例子 Another activity which/that I have chosen is photography.。出處

what 帶出的子句當主詞、受詞的完整用法在〈名詞子句〉那課。

例句：

  • What the client wants is a faster delivery time.（客戶要的是更快的交期。）
  • This is not what we ordered.（這不是我們訂的東西。）
  • These are the samples that we ordered.（這些就是我們訂的樣品。）
  • Everything that you need is in this folder.（你需要的東西都在這個資料夾裡。）

______ the client wants is a faster delivery time.

  • Which
  • That
  • What
  • Whose

前面沒有先行詞，「客戶要的東西」＝the thing that the client wants，用 What。

I'm afraid this is not ______ we ordered.

  • what
  • which
  • that
  • whose

is not 後面沒有名詞當先行詞，「我們訂的東西」用 what。which、that 前面要有先行詞。

These are the samples ______ we ordered last month.

  • what
  • whose
  • where
  • that

前面有先行詞 samples，用 that。what 前面不能有先行詞。

We will provide ______ you need for the presentation.

  • which
  • what
  • that
  • whose

provide 後面沒有名詞，「你需要的東西」用 what。

Everything ______ you need for the workshop is in this folder.

  • what
  • whose
  • that
  • where

前面有先行詞 Everything，用 that。劍橋說有先行詞時用 which 或 that，不用 what。

The supplier did not deliver ______ it had promised.

  • which
  • who
  • where
  • what

deliver 後面沒有名詞，「它承諾的東西」用 what。

關係子句裡的動詞，以及縮短

動詞跟先行詞一致；縮成分詞或不定詞片語

〈代名詞與主詞動詞一致〉講過：一句話裡有關係子句時，就有兩組主詞與動詞。隆德大學學術英文寫作網站：「每個動詞都要跟它所屬子句的主詞一致」（Each predicate verb must agree with the subject of the clause to which it belongs），它的例子 my mother, who has four siblings, loves me 裡，has 跟 who 一致，而 who 指的就是 my mother，所以是第三人稱單數。出處 所以關係子句裡的動詞，要看 who／which／that 指的是哪個名詞：

  • Employees who work overtime must record their hours.（who 指 employees，複數：work）
  • Each applicant who passes the test will be interviewed.（who 指 applicant，單數：passes）
  • The report that was sent to the board contained an error.（that 指 report，單數：was）
  • A customer whose orders are late will receive a discount.（關係子句的主詞是 whose orders，複數：are）

縮短成分詞或不定詞片語

劍橋：「關係子句的主詞跟主要子句的主詞相同時，關係子句可以是非限定的」（A relative clause can be non-finite when the subject of the relative clause is the same as the subject of the main clause），例如 The man sitting on the sofa over there is Simon’s brother.（＝who is sitting）、Don’t forget to fill in the form attached to the letter.（＝which is attached）；同一頁也有不定詞的例子 The person to ask about going to New Zealand is Beck.。出處 分詞片語怎麼縮，在〈分詞與分詞構句〉那課。

Employees who ______ overtime must record their hours in the system.

  • work
  • works
  • working
  • to work

who 指 employees（複數），用 work。working、to work 不是有時態的動詞，關係子句裡要有一個。

The report that ______ sent to the board yesterday contained an error.

  • were
  • was
  • is
  • be

that 指 report（單數），yesterday 是過去，報告是被寄出的：was sent。

Each applicant who ______ the written test will be invited to an interview.

  • pass
  • passing
  • passes
  • have passed

who 指 each applicant（單數），用 passes。have passed 是複數的形式。

The boxes which ______ on the loading dock need to be moved before noon.

  • is
  • was
  • has been
  • are

which 指 boxes（複數），用 are。

A customer whose orders ______ late will receive a discount on the next purchase.

  • is
  • are
  • has
  • was

關係子句的主詞是 whose orders（複數），不是 customer，用 are。

The people ______ the seminar should register at the front desk.

  • attend
  • attends
  • attending
  • attended

沒有 who，關係子句縮成分詞片語：attending the seminar＝who are attending the seminar（主動用 -ing）。attend、attends、attended 會讓句子出現兩個有時態的動詞卻沒有連接。

The person ______ about refunds is Ms. Diaz in Customer Service.

  • to contact
  • contact
  • contacts
  • will contact

不定詞片語放在名詞後面，意思是「該聯絡的那個人」，跟劍橋的 The person to ask about going to New Zealand is Beck. 同一個寫法。contact、contacts、will contact 會讓句子多出一個有時態的動詞卻沒有連接。

重點整理

  • 先行詞是人用 who，是東西用 which，that 兩種都行（只限沒有逗號的子句）；關係代名詞已經是主詞或受詞，子句裡不再加 he、it。
  • 看空格後面：直接接動詞＝缺主詞（who、which、that，whom 不行）；接主詞＋動詞而缺受詞＝受詞（who、whom、which、that 都可以）。受詞的關係代名詞在限定子句裡可以省略，主詞的不能省。
  • whose＋名詞＝「……的」，人和東西都能用；接名詞用 whose，接動詞用 who；who's 是 who is。
  • 非限定子句：前後加逗號、不能用 that、不能省略；有逗號是補充，沒逗號是「只有那些」。which 可以指前面整句話（, which surprised everyone）；數量詞＋of whom／of which。
  • 介系詞放前面時用 to whom、in which，不用 who、that；where、when、why＝at／on／for which。空格後面是完整的子句用 where、when，缺受詞用 which、that（the hotel where we stayed／the hotel which we recommended）。
  • what＝the thing that，前面沒有先行詞；前面有名詞就用 which、that。關係子句的動詞跟先行詞一致（employees who work、each applicant who passes），whose 子句跟 whose 後面的名詞一致。
